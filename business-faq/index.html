    Business Owner FAQs

    Clear answers about listing your business, claiming your profile, verifying ownership, posting events, offering deals, receiving inquiries, and partnering with Brunswick County Index.

    Adding Your Business

    4 Questions

    How do I add my business to Brunswick County Index?

    Submit your business through the Add Your Business page. You provide your business name, contact email, category, and community. There is no cost for a basic listing. After submission, our team reviews the information before publishing.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Is there a cost to list my business?

    No. Every Brunswick County business is eligible for a free directory listing with name, category, location, description, and contact information. Paid tiers add features like featured placement, deal publishing, and enhanced photos, but they are optional.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    What information appears on my business profile?

    Profiles display your business name, category, description, address, phone, website, hours, service area, accessibility information, social links, photos, and any active offers. You control which fields are populated after claiming your listing.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    How long does it take for a new listing to appear?

    New submissions enter a moderation queue and are typically reviewed within 2–3 business days. You receive a confirmation email when your listing is approved and published. Incomplete submissions may take longer.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Claiming Your Listing

    3 Questions

    How do I claim my existing business listing?

    Find your business profile and click "Claim This Business." Verify ownership using one of four methods: business email, phone call, website domain verification, or business document upload. Once verified, you gain full editing access.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    What if someone else claimed my business listing?

    Contact us immediately through the correction request page or contact form. Provide proof of ownership (business license, utility bill, or domain verification). Our team reviews the claim and transfers ownership if your documentation is confirmed.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Do I need to pay to claim my listing?

    No. Claiming and verifying your business listing is free. You can update hours, add photos, correct information, and respond to inquiries at no cost. Paid tiers are available for businesses that want enhanced visibility and additional features.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Updating & Correcting Info

    3 Questions

    How do I correct inaccurate information on my listing?

    Use the "Update This Listing" or "Report Info" link on any business profile. Submit a correction request with the updated details. Corrections are free and do not require a paid plan. Our team reviews and applies changes within 2–3 business days.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Can I update my hours, phone number, and address myself?

    Yes. Once you have claimed and verified your listing, you can update hours, phone, address, website, description, photos, and service area directly from your dashboard. Changes are published after a brief moderation check.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    What happens if my business closes or relocates?

    Submit a correction request to update your status or address. If your business permanently closes, we mark the listing as closed rather than deleting it, preserving historical information. Relocations update the address and map location.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Posting Events

    3 Questions

    How do I post an event on Brunswick County Index?

    Submit events through the Submit an Event page. Include the event title, date, start and end times, venue, address, organizer, description, ticket link, cost, and accessibility information. Events enter moderation before publication.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Are event listings free?

    Yes. Community events, festivals, markets, and gatherings are listed free of charge. All submitted events are reviewed by our moderation team to ensure they meet community guidelines before appearing on the public calendar.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Can I list a recurring event?

    Yes. When submitting your event, specify the recurrence pattern (daily, weekly, monthly). We create one canonical event record with individual occurrence dates, so your event appears on each scheduled date without creating duplicate pages.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    What does the "Verified Listing" badge mean?

    A Verified Listing badge indicates that the business owner has completed our ownership verification process and that the listing information has been reviewed by our team. It does not constitute an endorsement or guarantee of business quality.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I get my listing verified?

    Claim your listing and complete one of four verification methods: business email confirmation, phone call verification, website domain verification, or document upload. Once verified, the badge appears on your profile.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Is my verification data kept private?

    Yes. All verification documents, phone numbers, and email addresses used for verification are kept private and are never published on your public profile. Only the verification badge status is displayed publicly.
