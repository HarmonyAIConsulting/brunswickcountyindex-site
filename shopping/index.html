    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about shopping in Ocean Isle, Southport, Shallotte, and coastal NC.

    What are the primary shopping districts in Brunswick County, NC?

    Brunswick County offers distinct shopping hubs: Historic Downtown Southport for antiques, fine art galleries, and clothing boutiques; Ocean Isle Beach and Sunset Beach causeway districts for beachwear, gifts, and surf gear; Shallotte for major retail plazas and specialty home stores; and Calabash for nautical imports, artisan gifts, and specialty seafood markets.

    Where can I find locally made NC products and coastal crafts?

    You can discover authentic local art, handcrafted sea glass jewelry, pottery, and local honey at the Southport Artisan Market, Ocean Isle Beach Farmers & Crafters Market, Calabash Waterfront shops, and numerous independent galleries across Southport, Oak Island, and Leland.

    Are stores in Brunswick County open year-round?

    Yes! While some seasonal beach outfitters on the island causeways adjust their hours during winter months (November through February), the vast majority of boutiques, antique shops, hardware stores, and specialty retailers in Southport, Shallotte, Leland, and Oak Island operate 12 months a year.

    Where is the best place for antique shopping in the county?

    Historic Downtown Southport is widely recognized as the antique capital of coastal North Carolina. Stroll down Howe Street and Moore Street to explore multi-dealer antique malls, vintage bookshops, and estate jewelry stores tucked inside historic 19th-century buildings.

    What are the typical operating hours for local boutiques?

    Most local boutiques and specialty shops open around 10:00 AM and close between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM Monday through Saturday. During summer peak season, many island causeway stores stay open until 8:00 PM or 9:00 PM to accommodate vacationers.

    Are there weekly farmers and crafters markets during summer?

    Yes! Brunswick County hosts vibrant summer markets: Ocean Isle Beach Farmers Market (Tuesdays at OIB Park), Southport Waterfront Park Market (Wednesdays), Shallotte Farmers Market (Saturdays), and Sunset Beach Market (Thursdays at Sunset Beach Park).
