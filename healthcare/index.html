    Healthcare FAQs in Brunswick County

    Direct answers to common questions about local emergency care, specialists, senior health, and insurance.

    What are the primary hospitals serving Brunswick County, North Carolina?

    Brunswick County is anchored by two main acute care hospital systems: Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center located in Supply, NC (a 74-bed full-service hospital offering a 24/7 Emergency Department, maternity care, surgical suites, and intensive care), and Dosher Memorial Hospital located in historic Southport, NC (a critical access hospital providing 24/7 emergency care, outpatient surgery, cardiac rehabilitation, and diagnostic imaging).

    Where can visitors and residents find urgent care clinics in Brunswick County?

    Urgent care clinics are strategically located throughout Brunswick County for non-life-threatening emergencies. Major urgent care centers include Novant Health Express Care in Leland and Shallotte, EmergeOrtho Urgent Care in Shallotte and Leland for bone/joint injuries, and Dosher Urgent Care in Southport and Oak Island. Most clinics are open 7 days a week with walk-in service and on-site X-ray capability.

    How do retirees and seniors access healthcare services in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County offers robust senior healthcare infrastructure. Organizations like Brunswick Senior Resources, Inc. (BSRI) operate senior centers in Shallotte, Southport, Leland, Calabash, and Supply offering wellness programs, meal delivery, and health screenings. Major primary care practices accept Medicare and Medicare Advantage plans, while home health agencies provide in-home nursing and physical therapy.

    What emergency response resources exist for barrier island beach communities?

    Every barrier island (Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, Bald Head Island) maintains dedicated Municipal EMS and Fire Departments trained in coastal ocean rescues and cardiac emergencies. Emergency calls (911) dispatch paramedic ambulances directly. For Bald Head Island, emergency EMT transport coordinates with dedicated medical response vessels or helicopter transport when mainland hospitalization is required.

    Are specialized medical treatments available locally or do patients travel to Wilmington or Myrtle Beach?

    Brunswick County handles over 85% of standard medical needs locally, including cardiology, orthopedics, oncology, general surgery, gastroenterology, and pediatrics. For ultra-specialized tertiary trauma care or advanced pediatric intensive care, local physicians coordinate with NHRMC / Novant Health Wilmington (20-40 minutes north) or Grand Strand Medical Center in Myrtle Beach (30-45 minutes south).

    How can I find a dentist, optometrist, or primary care doctor accepting new patients?

    Brunswick County Index maintains a verified directory of local physicians, dentists, and health specialists. You can filter providers by community (Shallotte, Leland, Southport, OIB) or specialty, view verified ratings, and contact practices directly to confirm insurance coverage and new patient appointment availability.
