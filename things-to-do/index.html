    Activity Planning Quick Answers

    What are the top things to do in Brunswick County?

    Popular activities include climbing Oak Island Lighthouse and Old Baldy, walking to the Kindred Spirit Mailbox on Bird Island, exploring the NC Maritime Museum in Southport, fishing from ocean piers, kayaking salt marshes, golfing 30+ courses, and attending the NC Oyster Festival.

    Source: Visit NC Brunswick IslandsVisit NC Brunswick IslandsReviewed 2026-07-01

    What can I do in Brunswick County on a rainy day?

    Indoor options include Ingram Planetarium in Sunset Beach, the Museum of Coastal Carolina in Ocean Isle Beach, the NC Maritime Museum in Southport, Brunswick Town/Fort Anderson State Historic Site visitor center, Silver Coast Winery, and local art galleries in Southport and Shallotte.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Can you climb the lighthouses in Brunswick County?

    Yes. Oak Island Lighthouse offers guided climbs by reservation — it has 131 ship-ladder-style steps. Old Baldy on Bald Head Island is open for self-guided climbs with 108 spiral steps. Both require sturdy footwear; age restrictions may apply.

    Source: Oak Island LighthouseOak Island Lighthouse SocietyReviewed 2026-07-01

    Are there free things to do in Brunswick County?

    Yes. Free activities include beach access and swimming, walking the Kindred Spirit Mailbox trail on Bird Island, visiting the Green Swamp Preserve, Southport historic district walking tours, summer park concerts, and sea turtle education talks during nesting season.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Are Brunswick County beaches wheelchair accessible?

    Yes. Most beaches have ADA-accessible boardwalks and mobi-mats. Free beach wheelchairs with balloon tires are available at town halls and fire departments on Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, and Oak Island. Contact the town in advance to reserve one.

    Source: Town of Oak Island, NCTown of Oak IslandReviewed 2026-07-01

    Is Bald Head Island accessible for visitors with mobility needs?

    The passenger ferry from Southport is wheelchair accessible. Golf cart transport is available on the island, and several boardwalks to the beach are ADA-compliant. Old Baldy lighthouse climb is not wheelchair accessible due to narrow spiral stairs.

    Source: Bald Head Island FerryBald Head Island TransportationReviewed 2026-07-01

    Do Brunswick County festivals accommodate disabilities?

    Major festivals like the NC Oyster Festival and Southport 4th of July provide accessible parking, wheelchair-friendly walkways, and ASL interpreters at main stages. Check individual event listings for specific accessibility details.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Do I need to pay for beach parking in Brunswick County?

    Most barrier island towns charge for parking during peak season (typically April/May through September/October) via mobile app or meter. Off-season parking is free. Bald Head Island has no cars — you park at Deep Point Marina in Southport.

    Source: Town of Ocean Isle Beach, NCTown of Ocean Isle BeachReviewed 2026-07-01

    Where do I park for major festivals like the NC Oyster Festival?

    Major festivals provide satellite parking lots with free or low-cost shuttle buses running continuously to event gates. Accessible parking is available near main entrances. Check individual event listings on Brunswick County Index for specific parking maps.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Is parking available at Brunswick County beach accesses?

    Yes. Each barrier island has clearly marked public access points with nearby parking. Ocean Isle Beach has 30+ accesses along East and West Drive. Oak Island has 65+ accesses along Beach Drive. Some lots fill early on summer weekends — arrive before 10 AM for the best spots.

    Source: Town of Ocean Isle Beach, NCTown of Ocean Isle BeachReviewed 2026-07-01

    What happens if it rains during my beach vacation?

    Rainy day options include Ingram Planetarium, the Museum of Coastal Carolina, the NC Maritime Museum, Silver Coast Winery, local art galleries, and shopping in Southport or Shallotte. Summer thunderstorms typically pass within an hour. Check weather apps for rip current warnings.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    When is hurricane season in Brunswick County?

    Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30, with peak activity in September. Brunswick County has evacuation routes for barrier islands. Monitor the National Weather Service and Brunswick County Emergency Services for alerts. Travel insurance is recommended for fall trips.

    Source: National Weather ServiceNOAA National Weather ServiceReviewed 2026-07-01

    What is the best time of year to visit Brunswick County?

    May, September, and October offer warm weather, smaller crowds, and lower rental rates. Summer (June–August) is peak season with all attractions open. Winter is quiet with mild temperatures ideal for golf, birding, and shelling. Spring brings blooming azaleas and comfortable golf weather.

    Source: Visit NC Brunswick IslandsVisit NC Brunswick IslandsReviewed 2026-07-01

    What should I do if there is a rip current?

    If caught in a rip current, do not panic. Swim parallel to the shoreline until you are out of the current, then swim toward land. If you cannot escape, float on your back and wave for help. Always check posted rip current flags before entering the water.

    Source: National Weather ServiceNOAA National Weather ServiceReviewed 2026-07-01
    Visitor Intelligence

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What are the top must-see attractions in Brunswick County, North Carolina?

    The top must-see attractions in Brunswick County include the Kindred Spirit Mailbox on Bird Island (Sunset Beach), the 153-foot Oak Island Lighthouse (Caswell Beach), historic Old Baldy Lighthouse (Bald Head Island), the historic waterfront and yacht basin in Southport, the Museum of Coastal Carolina (Ocean Isle Beach), Ingram Planetarium (Sunset Beach), Brunswick Town / Fort Anderson State Historic Site (Winnabow), and the World Famous Calabash Seafood Waterfront.

    Are the beaches in Brunswick County free and publicly accessible?

    Yes! All 45+ miles of oceanfront beaches across Brunswick County's barrier islands—Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, Caswell Beach, and Bald Head Island—are public. Most offer clearly marked public beach access walkways with restrooms, outdoor showers, and handicap access ramps. Parking rules and paid parking schedules vary by municipality during peak summer months.

    What are the best indoor activities for a rainy day in Brunswick County?

    Great rainy day activities in Brunswick County include exploring the 50-foot dome star shows at Ingram Planetarium in Sunset Beach, touching marine sea life at the Museum of Coastal Carolina on Ocean Isle Beach, touring the NC Maritime Museum in historic Southport, visiting local art galleries and antique malls in Shallotte and Southport, or enjoying wine tastings at Silver Coast Winery.

    How do I visit Bald Head Island and Old Baldy Lighthouse?

    Bald Head Island is accessible via a passenger ferry departing from Deep Point Marina in Southport, NC. The ferry ride across the Cape Fear River takes approximately 20 minutes. Cars are not permitted on Bald Head Island; visitors travel by golf cart, bicycle, or foot. Tickets can be purchased at Deep Point Marina, and Old Baldy Lighthouse is a short walk or cart ride from the island ferry terminal.

    What family-friendly and kid-approved activities are available?

    Brunswick County offers abundant family activities including mini-golf courses in Ocean Isle and Sunset Beach, touch aquariums at the Museum of Coastal Carolina, pirate cruise excursions out of Southport, paddleboard and kayak rentals, ocean pier fishing, ice cream parlors, splash pads at Town Park in Leland, and loggerhead sea turtle nest watches.

    Can you climb the Oak Island Lighthouse?

    Yes, tours to climb the Oak Island Lighthouse gallery deck are available by advance reservation through the Oak Island Lighthouse official website. Unlike traditional spiral staircases, Oak Island Lighthouse features 131 steep, ship-ladder-style steps. Tour climbers must be at least 9 years old and wear rubber-soled shoes. The exterior grounds and boardwalk are open to the public daily.

    What are the best places for nature, bird watching, and wildlife?

    Brunswick County features extraordinary coastal biodiversity. Top wildlife spots include the 1,200-acre Bird Island Coastal Reserve in Sunset Beach (home to nesting loggerhead sea turtles and rare piping plovers), Green Swamp Preserve (home to native Venus flytraps and orchids), Bald Head Island Maritime Forest Reserve, and the Ev-Henwood Nature Preserve in Bolivia.

    Are dogs and pets allowed on Brunswick County beaches?

    Pet policies vary by town and season. Generally, dogs are allowed on leashes (typically 10-foot max) on most beaches during early morning and evening hours in peak summer months (May–September), and all day on leashes during the off-season. Always check specific municipal beach rules for Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, and Oak Island.
