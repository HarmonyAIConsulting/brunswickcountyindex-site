    VERIFIED PUBLIC INFORMATION

    Government & Public Services FAQ

    Answers to common resident, property owner, contractor, and visitor questions about Brunswick County government.

    Where is the Brunswick County Government Center located?

    The primary Brunswick County Government Complex is located at 30 Government Center Drive NE, Bolivia, NC 28422 (off Highway 17). It houses the Board of Commissioners, County Manager, Tax Office, Register of Deeds, Planning, Building Inspections, Public Utilities, Social Services, Health Department, and the Sheriff's Office.

    Source: Brunswick County GovernmentBrunswick County GovernmentReviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I set up water and sewer service in Brunswick County?

    County water and sewer service is managed by Brunswick County Public Utilities. New customers can set up service online via the county website or in person at the Utilities Department in Bolivia. Municipalities like Leland, Southport, and Oak Island operate their own municipal utility systems — check with your specific town hall.

    Source: Brunswick County Public UtilitiesBrunswick County Public UtilitiesReviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I register to vote or update my voter registration in Brunswick County?

    Voter registration is handled by the Brunswick County Board of Elections office located at 75 Stamp Act Dr NE, Building H, Bolivia, NC 28422. You can register or update your address online through the NC DMV portal, download a paper form, or register in person during designated early voting periods.

    Source: Brunswick County Board of ElectionsBrunswick County Board of ElectionsReviewed 2026-07-01

    Where do I pay my Brunswick County real estate and property taxes?

    County property taxes can be paid online via the Brunswick County Tax Collector portal, by mail, or in person at the Tax Office inside the Government Center in Bolivia. Annual tax bills are mailed in August and are due by September 1st (payable without interest through January 5th).

    Source: Brunswick County Tax OfficeBrunswick County Tax OfficeReviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I obtain a building permit or CAMA permit for coastal construction?

    Building permits are issued by the Brunswick County Code Enforcement department for unincorporated areas, or by the municipal building department if your property is within town limits (e.g., Leland, Ocean Isle Beach, Southport). Projects near coastal waters may also require a CAMA (Coastal Area Management Act) minor permit issued through town/county CAMA officers.

    Source: Brunswick County Planning & InspectionsBrunswick County Planning and InspectionsReviewed 2026-07-01

    What emergency alert system does Brunswick County use for hurricane warnings?

    Brunswick County Emergency Management utilizes 'ReadyBrunswick' powered by CodeRED to deliver urgent emergency alerts via phone call, text message, and email for hurricane evacuations, severe weather, boil water advisories, and public safety warnings. Residents and visitors can sign up for free online.

    Source: Brunswick County Emergency ManagementBrunswick County Emergency ManagementReviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I find my hurricane evacuation zone in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County uses state-designated evacuation zones (Zone A, Zone B, Zone C, etc.) based on storm surge risk. Zone A includes barrier islands (Ocean Isle, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, Bald Head) and low-lying coastal areas. You can view the interactive GIS evacuation map on the county emergency management website.

    Source: NC Department of Public Safety — Know Your ZoneNC Department of Public SafetyReviewed 2026-07-01

    Where are the DMV driver license and vehicle tag offices located?

    Full-service NC DMV driver license offices are located in Shallotte (West Brunswick), Leland, and Wilmington. Vehicle Tag & Title contract offices are located in Shallotte and Leland. Note that driver license and vehicle tag offices operate separately in North Carolina.

    Source: NC DMVNC Division of Motor VehiclesReviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I access public beach access points and boat ramps?

    Brunswick County features over 100 public beach access points across Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, Caswell Beach, and Bald Head Island, along with 12 NC Wildlife public boat ramps. Complete parking maps, handicap-accessible ramps, and facility details are listed on Brunswick County Index's Beaches and Boating pages.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Where can I recycle household waste in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County operates a main landfill in Supply along with 4 Convenience Centers (Leland, Southport, Supply, and Shallotte) that accept household trash, recyclables, yard waste, electronics, scrap metal, and hazardous household materials. Curbside recycling is also available through county and municipal waste contractors.

    Source: Brunswick County Solid WasteBrunswick County Solid Waste and RecyclingReviewed 2026-07-01
