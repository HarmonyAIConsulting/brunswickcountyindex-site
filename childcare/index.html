    Childcare FAQs in Brunswick County

    Direct answers to common questions about finding, evaluating, and affording childcare in Brunswick County.

    How do I find licensed childcare in Brunswick County, NC?

    Brunswick County Index maintains a directory of state-licensed childcare centers, family childcare homes, and preschools. You can filter by community, age group, and program type. To verify a provider's current NC Division of Child Development and Early Education (DCDEE) license status, use the NC Star-Rated License system. All licensed facilities undergo health and safety inspections, background checks, and staff training requirements.

    Source: NC DCDEENC Division of Child Development and Early EducationReviewed 2026-07-01

    What is NC Pre-K and how do I enroll my child in Brunswick County?

    NC Pre-K is a free, high-quality pre-kindergarten program for eligible four-year-old children. Brunswick County Schools operates NC Pre-K classrooms at elementary schools and partner childcare centers across the county. Enrollment priority is given to children from families meeting income eligibility guidelines. Contact Brunswick County Schools Early Childhood Education office for application materials and enrollment timelines.

    Source: NC Pre-K ProgramNC Division of Child Development and Early EducationReviewed 2026-07-01

    Is financial assistance available for childcare in Brunswick County?

    Yes. The NC Child Care Subsidy Program provides financial assistance for childcare to eligible low-income working families through the Brunswick County Department of Social Services. Smart Start of Brunswick County also offers supplemental funding and resources. Families must meet income and work/activity requirements. Contact Brunswick County DSS at (910) 253-2077 to apply.

    Source: Brunswick County DSSBrunswick County Department of Social ServicesReviewed 2026-07-01

    What should I look for when choosing a daycare or preschool in Brunswick County?

    Key factors include: NC DCDEE license with a star rating (1-5 stars), staff-to-child ratios appropriate for your child's age, curriculum and daily schedule, outdoor play space, nutrition and meal programs, cleanliness and safety protocols, staff qualifications and turnover rates, and parent communication practices. We recommend visiting multiple facilities, asking for references, and checking the NC DCDEE provider search tool for complaint history.

    Source: NC DCDEE Star-Rated LicenseNC Division of Child Development and Early EducationReviewed 2026-07-01

    Are there summer camps and programs for children in Brunswick County?

    Yes. Brunswick County offers numerous summer programs including Brunswick County Schools summer enrichment, YMCA day camps, church-based vacation Bible schools, sports camps at local parks, nature camps at Green Swamp Preserve and Bald Head Island, and beach-oriented programs. Most registration opens in March-April. Check our Things To Do section and Events calendar for seasonal program announcements.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Where can I find after-school care for my child in Brunswick County?

    After-school programs are available at most Brunswick County elementary schools through the school district's After-School Care program. Additional options include YMCA after-school care, Boys & Girls Club programs, church-based programs, and private daycare centers offering school-age care with transportation from local schools. Programs typically operate from school dismissal until 6:00 PM.

    Source: Brunswick County SchoolsBrunswick County SchoolsReviewed 2026-07-01
