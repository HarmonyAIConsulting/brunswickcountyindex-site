    Ocean Isle Beach, NC
    Family Vacations, Gentle Waves & Sunset Pier Walks

    Ocean Isle Beach

    7 Miles of Gentle Waves, Family Fun & Pier Sunsets

    Ocean Isle Beach is a beloved 7-mile barrier island featuring south-facing white sand beaches, gentle ocean surf, a historic oceanfront fishing pier, public beach accesses every block, and family-friendly dining.

    Parking: 500+ Public Spaces (Paid seasonal May–Sept, free off-season)
    Pet Rules: Leashed allowed off-peak hours (No dogs 9am-6pm May 15–Sept 15)
    Lifeguards: Oceanfront Patrols (Peak Summer)
    Accessibility: Handicap beach mats & free beach wheelchairs via Town Hall
    Access Points: 30+ Clearly marked public accesses with dune crossovers along East & West Drive.
    5.0 ★ (Natural Wonder)
    3 Miles Barrier Island + Bird Island Reserve ShorelineSouth-Facing (Sunrise & Sunset Ocean Views)
    Sunset Beach, NC
    Nature Lovers, Couples, Peace & Tranquility

    Sunset Beach

    Pristine Dunes, Bird Island Sanctuary & Kindred Spirit

    Consistently ranked among National Geographic's top beaches, Sunset Beach offers undeveloped sea oat dunes, wide firm white sands, the iconic Kindred Spirit Mailbox, and the 1,200-acre Bird Island Reserve.

    Parking: Main pier lot & street accesses (Paid schedule April–October)
    Pet Rules: Leashed allowed anytime except peak summer daytime (10am–5pm)
    Lifeguards: Roaming Water Rescue Patrols
    Accessibility: Mobi-Mat beach access & beach wheelchairs available at Pier
    Access Points: Main Pier access plus numbered street walkways along Main Street.
    4.8 ★ (Coastal Classic)
    8 Miles ShorelineSouth-Facing
    Holden Beach, NC
    Uncrowded Relaxation, Cottage Rentals & Waterway Boating

    Holden Beach

    Quiet Coastal Haven, ICW Waterway Access & Sea Turtles

    Ranked by National Geographic Traveler as one of the top family beaches in the country, Holden Beach features 8 miles of quiet residential shorelines, ICW boat docks, and dedicated sea turtle protection.

    Parking: Designated public lot under Holden Beach Bridge & street accesses
    Pet Rules: Leashed allowed anytime except 9am–5pm May 20–Sept 10
    Lifeguards: Ocean Rescue ATV Patrols
    Accessibility: Ramp crossovers at Bridge Park and select street accesses
    Access Points: Over 20 public walkways equipped with foot showers and trash receptacles.
    4.8 ★ (Beach Variety)
    10 Miles ShorelineSouth-Facing Barrier Coast
    Oak Island, NC
    Active Families, Anglers, Pet Owners & Lighthouse Buffs

    Oak Island

    10 Miles of Beaches, 2 Fishing Piers & Oak Island Lighthouse

    Oak Island boasts 10 miles of expansive coastline, two ocean fishing piers (Oak Island Pier & Ocean Crest Pier), public boat launches, splash pads, and the historic 153-foot Oak Island Lighthouse at Caswell Beach.

    Parking: 60+ Public beach accesses (Paid parking system with mobile app)
    Pet Rules: Dog friendly year-round! Leashed allowed 24/7; unleashed allowed Oct 15–Mar 15 (designated zones)
    Lifeguards: Seasonal Water Rescue Patrols
    Accessibility: ADA accessible ramps at 58th St, 40th St, and Middleton Park
    Access Points: 65+ public access walkways along Beach Drive with handicap parking.
    4.9 ★ (Historic & Quiet)
    3 Miles ShorelineSoutheast-Facing
    Caswell Beach, NC
    Quiet Walks, History Enthusiasts & Scenic River Views

    Caswell Beach

    Tranquil Shorelines, Oak Island Lighthouse & Golf Views

    Located at the eastern tip of Oak Island near the mouth of the Cape Fear River, Caswell Beach offers serene uncrowded sands, Oak Island Lighthouse climbs, and views of Old Baldy across the river.

    Parking: Lighthouse public access lot & regional beach access
    Pet Rules: Leashed allowed year-round
    Lifeguards: Mobile Water Patrol
    Accessibility: ADA boardwalk at Regional Access
    Access Points: Regional access lot with boardwalk crossovers and outdoor showers.
    5.0 ★ (Luxury Refuge)
    14 Miles across South Beach, East Beach & Cape Fear Point ShorelineMulti-facing (Gentle South Beach & Waves on East Beach)
    Bald Head Island, NC
    Luxury Escapes, Eco-Tourism & Romantic Getaways

    Bald Head Island

    Car-Free Paradise, Maritime Forests & Old Baldy Lighthouse

    Accessible only by passenger ferry from Southport, Bald Head Island features 14 miles of pristine uncrowded beaches, zero cars (golf carts only), maritime forest trails, and North Carolina's oldest lighthouse, Old Baldy.

    Parking: No cars on island; ferry parking at Deep Point Marina in Southport
    Pet Rules: Leashed allowed on ferry and island beaches
    Lifeguards: Private community patrols
    Accessibility: Golf cart transport & ADA accessible boardwalks
    Access Points: Dune boardwalk accesses winding through maritime forest habitats.

    Responsible Coastal Enjoyment

    Beach Safety & Ordinance Guide

    Help keep Brunswick County's beaches pristine, safe, and welcoming for everyone.

    Rip Current Safety

    Never fight a rip current. Swim parallel to the shoreline until out of the current, then turn toward land.

    Dune Protection

    Sea oats and sand dunes protect our island from erosion. Walking or climbing on dunes is strictly prohibited by NC law.

    Sea Turtle Nesting

    Keep beach lights off at night May–October. Fill in sand holes and level sandcastles before leaving the beach.

    Pet Leash Regulations

    Pets must remain on leashes on all beaches. Pay attention to summer daytime hours when pets are restricted.

    Leave No Trace

    Pack out all trash, pop-up canopies, umbrellas, and personal gear daily. Over-night equipment left on beach is impounded.

    Beach Wheelchairs

    Free beach wheelchairs with inflatable balloon tires are available through town halls and fire departments.

    Beach Visitor Quick Answers

    Are Brunswick County beaches free and open to the public?

    Yes. All 45+ miles of oceanfront on Brunswick County's six barrier islands — Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, Caswell Beach, and Bald Head Island — are public. Beach access walkways are free; some towns charge for parking during peak season.

    Source: Town of Ocean Isle Beach, NCTown of Ocean Isle BeachReviewed 2026-07-01

    Which Brunswick County beach is best for families with children?

    Ocean Isle Beach and Holden Beach are top choices for families. Both have gentle south-facing surf, gradual sandbars, public restrooms at piers, nearby mini-golf, and wide sandy stretches. Sunset Beach is quieter and ideal for nature-loving families.

    Source: Visit NC Brunswick IslandsVisit NC Brunswick IslandsReviewed 2026-07-01

    Can you see both sunrise and sunset over the ocean in Brunswick County?

    Yes. Because the barrier islands run east–west along a south-facing coastline, visitors can watch the sun rise and set over the Atlantic Ocean, especially from late fall through winter when the sun's angle aligns with the shoreline.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Are there lifeguards on Brunswick County beaches?

    Lifeguard and ocean rescue patrols operate during peak summer months on most beaches. Oak Island, Ocean Isle Beach, and Holden Beach have dedicated ocean rescue teams. Always check posted flags and swim near lifeguarded areas.

    Source: Oak Island Water RescueTown of Oak IslandReviewed 2026-07-01

    Are Brunswick County beaches wheelchair accessible?

    Yes. Most beaches have ADA-accessible boardwalks and mobi-mats. Free beach wheelchairs with balloon tires are available at town halls and fire departments on Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, and Oak Island. Contact the town in advance to reserve one.

    Source: Town of Oak Island, NCTown of Oak IslandReviewed 2026-07-01

    Is Bald Head Island accessible for visitors with mobility needs?

    The passenger ferry from Southport is wheelchair accessible. Golf cart transport is available on the island, and several boardwalks to the beach are ADA-compliant. Old Baldy lighthouse climb is not wheelchair accessible due to narrow spiral stairs.

    Source: Bald Head Island FerryBald Head Island TransportationReviewed 2026-07-01

    Do Brunswick County festivals accommodate disabilities?

    Major festivals like the NC Oyster Festival and Southport 4th of July provide accessible parking, wheelchair-friendly walkways, and ASL interpreters at main stages. Check individual event listings for specific accessibility details.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Can I bring my dog to Brunswick County beaches?

    Yes, but rules vary by town and season. Oak Island allows leashed dogs 24/7 year-round. Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, and Holden Beach restrict dogs during peak daytime hours in summer (typically 9 AM–6 PM, May–September). Leashes are required on all beaches.

    Source: Town of Oak Island, NCTown of Oak IslandReviewed 2026-07-01

    Are there off-leash areas for dogs on Brunswick County beaches?

    Oak Island permits off-leash dogs in designated zones from October 15 through March 15. All other beaches require leashes at all times. Service animals are exempt from leash restrictions under federal ADA law.

    Source: Town of Oak Island, NCTown of Oak IslandReviewed 2026-07-01

    Can I take my dog on the Bald Head Island ferry?

    Yes. Leashed dogs are welcome on the Bald Head Island passenger ferry from Southport. Dogs are also allowed on the island beaches while leashed. Bring proof of current vaccinations if requested.

    Source: Bald Head Island FerryBald Head Island TransportationReviewed 2026-07-01

    Do I need to pay for beach parking in Brunswick County?

    Most barrier island towns charge for parking during peak season (typically April/May through September/October) via mobile app or meter. Off-season parking is free. Bald Head Island has no cars — you park at Deep Point Marina in Southport.

    Source: Town of Ocean Isle Beach, NCTown of Ocean Isle BeachReviewed 2026-07-01

    Where do I park for major festivals like the NC Oyster Festival?

    Major festivals provide satellite parking lots with free or low-cost shuttle buses running continuously to event gates. Accessible parking is available near main entrances. Check individual event listings on Brunswick County Index for specific parking maps.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    Is parking available at Brunswick County beach accesses?

    Yes. Each barrier island has clearly marked public access points with nearby parking. Ocean Isle Beach has 30+ accesses along East and West Drive. Oak Island has 65+ accesses along Beach Drive. Some lots fill early on summer weekends — arrive before 10 AM for the best spots.

    Source: Town of Ocean Isle Beach, NCTown of Ocean Isle BeachReviewed 2026-07-01

    What happens if it rains during my beach vacation?

    Rainy day options include Ingram Planetarium, the Museum of Coastal Carolina, the NC Maritime Museum, Silver Coast Winery, local art galleries, and shopping in Southport or Shallotte. Summer thunderstorms typically pass within an hour. Check weather apps for rip current warnings.

    Source: Brunswick County IndexBrunswick County IndexReviewed 2026-07-01

    When is hurricane season in Brunswick County?

    Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30, with peak activity in September. Brunswick County has evacuation routes for barrier islands. Monitor the National Weather Service and Brunswick County Emergency Services for alerts. Travel insurance is recommended for fall trips.

    Source: National Weather ServiceNOAA National Weather ServiceReviewed 2026-07-01

    What is the best time of year to visit Brunswick County?

    May, September, and October offer warm weather, smaller crowds, and lower rental rates. Summer (June–August) is peak season with all attractions open. Winter is quiet with mild temperatures ideal for golf, birding, and shelling. Spring brings blooming azaleas and comfortable golf weather.

    Source: Visit NC Brunswick IslandsVisit NC Brunswick IslandsReviewed 2026-07-01

    What should I do if there is a rip current?

    If caught in a rip current, do not panic. Swim parallel to the shoreline until you are out of the current, then swim toward land. If you cannot escape, float on your back and wave for help. Always check posted rip current flags before entering the water.

    Source: National Weather ServiceNOAA National Weather ServiceReviewed 2026-07-01
    Beach Visitor FAQs

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which beach in Brunswick County is best for families with young children?

    Ocean Isle Beach and Holden Beach are widely considered the best for young families. Both feature gentle south-facing ocean surf, shallow sloping water bars, public restrooms at oceanfront piers, playground parks, mini-golf courses, ice cream shops, and wide sand beaches.

    How do I get to the Kindred Spirit Mailbox on Bird Island?

    To reach the Kindred Spirit Mailbox, park at the West End beach access on Sunset Beach (at the end of West Main Street). Walk west along the sand for approximately 1.5 miles onto Bird Island Reserve. You will find the wooden mailbox nestled in the sand dunes with notebooks and pens for visitors.

    Are dogs allowed on Brunswick County beaches?

    Yes! Dogs are welcome on all Brunswick County beaches, but rules vary by town and season. Oak Island is the most pet-friendly, allowing leashed dogs 24/7 year-round. Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, and Holden Beach allow leashed dogs during early morning and late afternoon hours in summer, and all day during the off-season.

    Are public beach accesses free and do I need to pay for parking?

    All beach access walkways in Brunswick County are free to the public. Paid parking systems operate seasonally (typically April/May through September/October) at Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, and Oak Island via mobile app or parking meters. Off-season parking is free.

    Can you see both the sunrise and sunset over the ocean on Brunswick County beaches?

    Yes! Because Brunswick County's barrier islands (Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, and Oak Island) run east-west along a south-facing coastline, visitors can witness both ocean sunrises and ocean sunsets during late autumn and winter months.

    Where can I borrow a free beach wheelchair?

    Free beach wheelchairs equipped with balloon tires are available on Ocean Isle Beach (Town Hall / Water Rescue), Sunset Beach (Sunset Beach Pier & Town Hall), Holden Beach (Town Hall), and Oak Island (Parks & Rec / Ocean Rescue).
