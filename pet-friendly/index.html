    BEACH LEASH RULES

    Dog Beach Rules by Town

    Each barrier island sets its own seasonal pet policy. Check before you go.

    Ocean Isle Beach

    Leashed dogs allowed. Prohibited 9am–6pm May 15–Sept 15.

    Sunset Beach

    Leashed dogs allowed anytime except 10am–5pm peak summer.

    Holden Beach

    Leashed dogs allowed. Prohibited 9am–5pm May 20–Sept 10.

    Oak Island

    Leashed dogs allowed year-round on beaches. Clean up required.

    Bald Head Island

    Leashed dogs welcome. Ferry allows pets. Beach access year-round.

    Southport

    Leashed dogs welcome on waterfront boardwalk and parks.

    PET TRAVEL FAQS

    Pet-Friendly Travel Questions

    Direct answers about traveling with pets to Brunswick County.

    Can I bring my dog to Brunswick County beaches?

    Yes, with restrictions. Each barrier island has seasonal leash rules. Ocean Isle Beach prohibits dogs 9am–6pm May 15–Sept 15. Sunset Beach restricts dogs 10am–5pm during peak summer. Holden Beach prohibits dogs 9am–5pm May 20–Sept 10. Oak Island allows leashed dogs year-round. Off-peak season (October–April), all beaches welcome leashed dogs. Always clean up after your pet.

    Are there off-leash dog parks in Brunswick County?

    Yes. Leland offers a fenced dog park at Westgate Nature Park. Shallotte has a dog park at Shallotte River Park. Oak Island has a designated off-leash area. Most are free and open dawn to dusk with separate sections for large and small dogs.

    Where can I find pet-friendly vacation rentals?

    Over 320 vacation rentals in Brunswick County welcome dogs. Search our lodging directory and filter for pet-friendly properties. Many beach houses in Ocean Isle, Sunset Beach, and Holden Beach offer fenced yards, dog wash stations, and proximity to pet-friendly beach access points.

    Are there pet-friendly restaurants in Brunswick County?

    Yes. Many waterfront restaurants with outdoor decks welcome leashed dogs, particularly in Southport (waterfront boardwalk), Ocean Isle (pier area), and Calabash (waterfront docks). Look for restaurants with patio seating. Call ahead to confirm pet policy.

    What veterinary services are available for visitors?

    Brunswick County has multiple veterinary clinics offering routine and emergency care, including facilities in Shallotte, Southport, Leland, and Supply. For after-hours emergencies, call ahead — some clinics offer 24/7 emergency service. Search our directory for "veterinary" to find nearby options.

    Can I take my dog on the Bald Head Island ferry?

    Yes. The Bald Head Island ferry allows leashed dogs on the passenger ferry. There is no additional pet fee. Dogs must remain leashed at all times on the ferry and on the island. Golf cart rentals (the primary island transportation) also accommodate leashed dogs.
