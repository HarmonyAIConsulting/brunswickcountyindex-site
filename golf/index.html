    GOLF ADVISORY & FAQS

    Frequently Asked Golf Questions

    Everything you need to know about tee times, dress codes, weather, golf carts, and trip planning in Brunswick County, NC.

    Why is Brunswick County called North Carolina's Golf Capital?

    Brunswick County boasts over 30 world-class golf courses designed by legendary architects like Arnold Palmer, Tim Cate, Fred Couples, Dan Maples, and Willard Byrd. Positioned between Wilmington, NC and Myrtle Beach, SC, Brunswick County offers pristine coastal layouts, year-round mild weather, and significantly less crowded fairways.

    What are the top public and resort golf courses in Brunswick County?

    Local courses open to public play include Leopard's Chase and Tiger's Eye at Ocean Ridge Plantation, Thistle Golf Club in Sunset Beach, Rivers Edge in Shallotte, Cape Fear National in Leland, Carolina National in Bolivia, and The Pearl Golf Links in Calabash.

    How far in advance should I book tee times in Brunswick County?

    For peak spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) golf seasons, it is highly recommended to book tee times 60 to 90 days in advance. Off-peak summer and winter tee times can usually be reserved 1 to 2 weeks ahead.

    Are golf packages and 'Stay and Play' options available?

    Yes! Dozens of local golf travel specialists and resorts—such as Ocean Ridge Plantation, Sea Trail Resort, and local rental agencies—offer customized Stay & Play packages combining luxury accommodations, preferred tee times, meals, and replay rounds.

    What is the dress code at Brunswick County golf courses?

    Most championship and resort courses require traditional golf attire: collared shirts or tailored mock necks for men, slacks or golf shorts, soft-spike or spikeless golf shoes, and no denim or athletic gym wear.

    Can I rent golf clubs and equipment locally?

    Yes. All major golf courses and dedicated local pro shops offer premium rental sets from top brands like Titleist, Callaway, and TaylorMade for men, women, and left-handed players.

    Are street-legal golf carts allowed on public roads near courses?

    Street-legal Low Speed Vehicles (LSVs) equipped with seatbelts, headlights, turn signals, and VIN numbers are permitted on municipal roads with posted speed limits of 35mph or lower across towns like Ocean Isle Beach, Oak Island, and Southport.

    Which golf courses offer the best water and marsh views?

    Rivers Edge in Shallotte features 7 holes along the tidal Shallotte River. Carolina National winds along the Lockwood Folly River, while Oak Island Golf Club sits right across the street from the Atlantic Ocean on Caswell Beach.
