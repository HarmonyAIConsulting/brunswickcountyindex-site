    TRIP PLANNING

    Family Planning Tips

    Beach Safety First

    Swim near lifeguarded areas, check rip-current flags, and bring reef-safe sunscreen. Free beach wheelchairs available at Ocean Isle Town Hall.

    Save on Attractions

    Combo tickets for Ingram Planetarium + Museum of Coastal Carolina save 20%. Many parks, trails, and beach access are free.

    Best Beaches for Kids

    Ocean Isle Beach has the gentlest waves and most amenities. Sunset Beach offers nature exploration. Holden Beach is quiet and uncrowded.

    Dine with Kids

    Most coastal restaurants are casual and family-welcoming. Look for waterfront spots with outdoor seating near piers for the best experience.

    FAMILY TRAVEL FAQS

    Family Travel Questions Answered

    Direct answers for planning your Brunswick County family vacation.

    What are the best family beaches in Brunswick County?

    Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, and Holden Beach are the most family-friendly. All three feature south-facing shorelines with gentle waves, public restrooms, nearby parking, and lifeguard patrols during peak summer. Ocean Isle Beach also offers free beach wheelchairs through Town Hall.

    Are there kid-friendly activities beyond the beach?

    Yes. Families can visit the Museum of Coastal Carolina (touch tanks), Ingram Planetarium (dome shows), Oak Island Lighthouse (climb for ages 7+), mini-golf courses, arcades, splash pads in Shallotte and Leland, dolphin-watching boat tours, and sea turtle nesting education programs. Brunswick Nature Park in Bolivia offers 10+ miles of trails suitable for strollers and kids.

    Where can families go fishing without a license?

    Fishing from a licensed ocean pier does not require an individual NC fishing license. Ocean Isle Fishing Pier, Sunset Beach Pier, Oak Island Pier, and Holden Beach Pier all welcome families and rent rods on-site. Surf fishing from the beach requires a NC Coastal Recreational Fishing License (available online for $5 for 10 days).

    What should families know about beach safety?

    Always swim near lifeguarded areas during peak summer. Watch for rip currents — if caught, swim parallel to shore until free. Apply reef-safe sunscreen, bring shade, and stay hydrated. Beach wheelchairs are available free at Ocean Isle Town Hall and Sunset Beach Pier. Water temperatures peak at 82°F in August.

    Are there free or low-cost family activities?

    Many. Beach access is free at all public points. Parks, playgrounds, nature trails, the Kindred Spirit Mailbox walk, lighthouse exterior viewing, and the Southport waterfront boardwalk are all free. The NC Maritime Museum at Southport charges a small admission; Ingram Planetarium and Museum of Coastal Carolina offer combo tickets.

    What is the best time of year for a family beach vacation?

    Late May through August offers warm water (78-82°F), lifeguards, and full seasonal operations. September and October are excellent for fewer crowds, lower rates, and warm weather. Spring (March-May) is pleasant but ocean water is cool. Winter is quiet with limited services but very low rates.
