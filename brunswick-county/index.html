    Brunswick County, North Carolina

    Twenty coastal communities. Forty-five miles of Atlantic shoreline. Thirty-plus golf courses winding through maritime forests. Historic ports, shrimp docks, and lighthouses. Brunswick County is one of the fastest-growing counties in North Carolina — and this is your complete guide to everything it offers.

    What Makes It Special

    Why Brunswick County?

    From the shrimp docks of Calabash to the oak-lined streets of Southport, Brunswick County blends coastal heritage with rapid growth and natural beauty.

    Six Barrier Islands

    Sunset Beach, Ocean Isle Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, Caswell Beach, and Bald Head Island — each with its own character, from tranquil bird sanctuaries to lively family beaches.

    Historic Maritime Heritage

    Southport's 18th-century port, Old Baldy Lighthouse on Bald Head Island, the Calabash seafood tradition, and Fort Johnston — the oldest active garrison in North Carolina.

    30+ Golf Courses

    Championship layouts by Palmer, Jones, and Maples wind through maritime forests and salt marshes, making Brunswick County a premier coastal golf destination.

    Nature & Preserves

    Green Swamp Preserve, Boiling Spring Lakes Conservation, Bald Head Woods Maritime Forest, and the Lockwoods Folly River — rare carnivorous plants, longleaf pines, and sea turtle nesting grounds.

    Fastest-Growing County in NC

    Leland, Belville, and the US-17 corridor are among the fastest-growing areas in the state, attracting families and retirees with coastal lifestyle and affordable living.

    20 Distinct Communities

    From the car-free luxury of Bald Head Island to the commercial hub of Shallotte, each town contributes its own story to the Brunswick County tapestry.

    Common Questions

    Brunswick County FAQ

    Where is Brunswick County, North Carolina?

    Brunswick County is the southernmost county in North Carolina, bordered by the Atlantic Ocean to the south, the Cape Fear River to the east, New Hanover County to the northeast, Columbus County to the north, and Horry County, South Carolina to the west. The county seat is Bolivia.

    What are the best beaches in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County has six barrier-island beach communities: Sunset Beach, Ocean Isle Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, Caswell Beach, and Bald Head Island. Each offers a distinct experience — from quiet family shores to resort-style amenities. Explore our Beaches & Parks page for detailed access points and conditions.

    How many towns are in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County has 20 incorporated municipalities and several census-designated places. Major communities include Shallotte (the commercial hub), Leland (the fastest-growing), Southport (the historic port), and Bolivia (the county seat). Browse all 20 communities on our directory.

    What is Brunswick County known for?

    Brunswick County is known as home to the "Seafood Capital of the World" (Calabash), 30+ championship golf courses, 45+ miles of coastline, historic lighthouses including Old Baldy, and being one of the fastest-growing counties in North Carolina.
