    Brunswick County’s Premier Residential & Business Growth Hub

    Incorporated in 1989, Leland, North Carolina has evolved from a quiet rural community into the most dynamic, rapidly growing town in the Cape Fear region. Strategically positioned directly across the Cape Fear and Brunswick Rivers from historic Wilmington, Leland combines modern suburban amenities with southern coastal charm.

    Why People Choose Leland

    • Master-Planned Resort Living: Home to nationally recognized communities like Brunswick Forest, Compass Pointe, and Waterford with world-class amenities.
    • Unbeatable Location: Just 10 minutes to downtown Wilmington, 25 minutes to coastal beaches, and seamless access to US-17, US-74, and I-140.
    • Thriving Business Environment: Rapidly expanding retail centers, healthcare facilities, dining establishments, and professional service firms.

    Living in Leland, NC

    Whether you are relocating to a resort golf community, starting a local business, or seeking top-tier healthcare and active outdoor parks, Leland provides an unmatched quality of life.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Leland, NC

    Helpful answers for newcomers, residents, home buyers, and visitors

    Why is Leland recognized as one of the fastest-growing towns in North Carolina?

    Leland combines a prime geographic location just 10 minutes from historic downtown Wilmington with modern infrastructure, top-ranked master-planned communities (Brunswick Forest, Compass Pointe, Waterford, Magnolia Greens), low county tax rates, excellent schools, and easy access to coastal NC beaches.

    Where is Leland located in Brunswick County?

    Leland is located in northern Brunswick County along the US Highway 17, US 74/76, and NC 133 corridors, positioned directly across the Cape Fear and Brunswick Rivers from Wilmington, NC.

    What master-planned communities are located in Leland?

    Leland is famous for premier master-planned communities including Brunswick Forest, Compass Pointe, Waterford of the Carolinas, Magnolia Greens, Mallard Bay, Seabrooke, and Windsor Park, featuring amenities such as championship golf, indoor/outdoor pools, pickleball courts, and miles of nature trails.

    What parks and nature trails are available in Leland?

    Key parks include Westgate Nature Park (with elevated wetland boardwalks), Founders Park (hosting amphitheater concerts and community gatherings), and the nearby Brunswick Riverwalk at Belville (offering river piers and boat launches).

    How far is Leland from the nearest beaches?

    Leland is conveniently located within 25 to 30 minutes of several top barrier island beaches, including Wrightsville Beach, Oak Island, Caswell Beach, and Holden Beach.

    What healthcare facilities serve the Leland community?

    Leland is anchored by Novant Health Leland Medical Plaza, EmergeOrtho, Wilmington Health Leland, and numerous specialized primary, dental, and urgent care centers, with full hospital care minutes away at Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center in Shallotte and Novant Health NHRMC in Wilmington.

    What cultural and arts venues exist in Leland?

    The town operates the Leland Cultural Arts Center, a 18,000-square-foot facility offering art courses, pottery studios, gallery exhibits, dance rooms, and a 250-seat theater for community plays and concerts.

    What golf courses are open to the public in Leland?

    Golfers in Leland can play Magnolia Greens Golf Course (a 27-hole public championship course that has hosted PGA Tour qualifying) and Cape Fear National at Brunswick Forest (an acclaimed 18-hole Tim Cate design).

    What river access points exist for boating and kayaking in Leland?

    Boaters and kayakers can access the water at Sturgeon Creek Park launch and the Brunswick Riverwalk pier in adjacent Belville, leading directly into the Cape Fear River and Intracoastal waterways.

    What public schools serve Leland students?

    Leland is served by Brunswick County Schools, including North Brunswick High School, Leland Middle School, Belville Elementary, Town Creek Elementary, and Lincoln Elementary, along with public charter schools like Charter Day School.

    What shopping centers are available in Leland?

    Shopping hubs include the Villages at Brunswick Forest, Waterford Commercial Center, Westgate Shopping Center, and Leland Town Center, featuring grocery stores (Harris Teeter, Publix, Aldi), pharmacies, clothing stores, and service businesses.

    Getting To & Around Leland

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-17/US-74/76 — the gateway to Wilmington from the west. ~10 min to downtown Wilmington, ~30 min to Oak Island beaches.

    Nearest Airport

    Wilmington International (ILM) — 15 miles east via US-74/76. Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 65 miles south.

    Parking

    Free parking at Westgate Nature Park, Founders Park, and all commercial centers along US-17. Ample lot parking throughout.

    Beach Access

    25-minute drive to Oak Island, Caswell Beach, and Wrightsville Beach via US-17 south or US-74/76 east.

    Boat Access

    Brunswick Riverwalk boat launch and kayak trail. Sturgeon Creek Park provides additional water access.

    Public Transit

    Wave Transit operates limited bus service connecting Leland to Wilmington. Personal vehicle recommended for most trips.

    Accessibility at Leland

    Westgate Nature Park

    Fully accessible elevated boardwalk trails with observation decks. Paved parking and ADA-compliant restrooms.

    Cultural Arts Center

    Leland Cultural Arts Center is fully accessible with ramped entry, elevator access, and accessible restrooms.

    Brunswick Riverwalk

    Accessible boardwalk and fishing pier with paved approaches. ADA-compliant parking available.

    Golf Courses

    Cape Fear National and Magnolia Greens offer accessible clubhouse facilities. Cart access for mobility devices with advance notice.
