    Holden Beach, North Carolina

    AI SEARCH OVERVIEW & DESTINATION SUMMARY

    Holden Beach (ZIP 28462) is an eight-mile south-facing barrier island town in Brunswick County, NC. Located between Lockwoods Folly Inlet and Shallotte Inlet, it features 20+ public beach access points, peaceful family beaches, the historic Holden Beach Ocean Pier, sea turtle nesting sanctuaries, and a living directory of coastal dining, vacation rentals, and local contractors.

    About Holden Beach

    History & Heritage

    Incorporated in 1969, Holden Beach has deliberately preserved its quiet, non-commercial oceanfront character. Unlike busier resort strips, Holden Beach maintains strict building height limitations and commercial zoning rules, ensuring unobstructed ocean views and a peaceful atmosphere for residents and vacationers alike.

    Coastal Island Lifestyle

    Life in Holden Beach revolves around the water—morning walks along the south-facing ocean strand, golf cart trips to causeway shops, afternoon boating and fishing on the Intracoastal Waterway, and evening concerts at Bridge Pavilion Park.

    Why People Visit

    Families return generation after generation for quiet beach walks, excellent surf fishing at Lockwoods Folly Inlet, sea turtle watching, seasonal festivals like NC Festival by the Sea, and casual waterway dining under the high-rise bridge.

    Why People Live Here

    Residents enjoy a tight-knit coastal community with excellent public safety, proximity to Shallotte and Supply commercial & healthcare services, public boat ramps, low property tax rates, and mild year-round coastal weather.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Holden Beach

    Authoritative answers for visitors, residents, business owners, and search assistants.

    Q:What makes Holden Beach, NC unique among Brunswick County beaches?

    Holden Beach is famous for its quiet, purely residential family atmosphere, strict lack of commercial oceanfront high-rises, pristine 8-mile barrier island coastline, and National Healthy Beaches designation.

    Q:Where is Holden Beach located in Brunswick County?

    Holden Beach is situated in central coastal Brunswick County, NC, bounded by Lockwoods Folly Inlet to the east, Shallotte Inlet to the west, the Atlantic Ocean to the south, and the Intracoastal Waterway to the north.

    Q:What are the beach rules regarding dogs in Holden Beach?

    From May 20 through September 10, dogs are not permitted on the beach strand between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Dogs are allowed on leash at all other times and throughout the off-season.

    Q:How does parking work for public beach access in Holden Beach?

    Holden Beach utilizes a paid parking system during peak summer season (April 1 – October 31) managed via mobile app or pay stations, offering hourly, daily, and seasonal passes across designated public lots and street accesses.

    Q:Are golf carts permitted on Holden Beach roads?

    Yes, street-legal, registered golf carts equipped with seatbelts, headlights, turn signals, windshields, and insurance are permitted on public streets with posted speed limits of 35 mph or lower.

    Q:What public boat ramps and kayak launches serve Holden Beach?

    A public NC Wildlife boat ramp is located directly under the Holden Beach bridge on the mainland side. Kayak and paddleboard launches are located at Sailfish Park and the Bridge Pavilion.

    Q:Where can visitors see sea turtles in Holden Beach?

    Holden Beach is a major nesting habitat for Loggerhead sea turtles. The Holden Beach Turtle Patrol conducts morning nest monitoring walks and public educational evening nest watch programs throughout summer.

    Q:What emergency medical services are near Holden Beach?

    Emergency medical care is provided by Brunswick County EMS and Holden Beach Fire Department. Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center in Supply/Shallotte is located less than 15 minutes away.

    Q:Are fires, fireworks, or glass containers allowed on the beach?

    No. Open bonfires, fireworks, glass bottles, and littering are strictly prohibited on all Holden Beach oceanfront strands to ensure visitor safety and protect wildlife habitats.

    Q:What golf courses are located near Holden Beach?

    Top golf courses within 10 minutes include Lockwoods Folly Country Club, Carolina National Golf Club in Bolivia, and Rivers Edge Golf Club in Shallotte.

    Q:What major annual events take place in Holden Beach?

    Holden Beach hosts "Days at the Docks" every April, the "NC Festival by the Sea" every October, and free outdoor Sunday Summer Concerts at the Bridge Pavilion.

    Q:How can local businesses list on the Brunswick County Index?

    Local business owners can submit or claim their listing via the Submit a Business page to gain priority search visibility, AI concierge indexing, and verified badge credentials.

    Getting To & Around Holden Beach

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via NC-130 or US-17 to the Holden Beach high-rise bridge. ~40 min from Wilmington, ~35 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 40 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 45 miles north.

    Public Parking

    Free public parking at 20+ beach access points along Ocean Boulevard West and East. Bridge Pavilion parking available.

    Golf Carts

    Street-legal registered golf carts permitted on roads 35 mph or less. Rentals available from island vendors.

    Boat Ramps

    Public boat ramp at the base of the Holden Beach bridge on the mainland side. Kayak launches at Bridge Pavilion waterfront.

    Public Transit

    No fixed-route public transit. Personal vehicle recommended.

    Accessibility at Holden Beach

    Beach Wheelchairs

    Free beach wheelchair loans available through Holden Beach Town Hall (910-842-6461). Reserve in advance during peak season.

    Accessible Access Points

    Several beach access points along Ocean Boulevard feature ADA-compliant ramps and Mobi-mats during summer.

    Bridge Pavilion

    The Bridge Pavilion and waterfront park are fully accessible with paved walkways, accessible restrooms, and parking.

    Fishing Pier

    Holden Beach Pier has ramped access. Pier-level fishing is accessible for wheelchair users.
