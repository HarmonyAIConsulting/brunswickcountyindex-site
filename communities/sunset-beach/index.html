    AI SEARCH OVERVIEW & DESTINATION SUMMARY

    Sunset Beach (ZIP 28468) is a tranquil 3-mile south-facing barrier island and mainland town in Brunswick County, NC. Famous for the Kindred Spirit Mailbox on Bird Island Coastal Reserve, Sunset Beach Fishing Pier, Ingram Planetarium, and championship golf courses, it provides an uncrowded sanctuary for residents, retirees, and coastal vacationers.

    About Sunset Beach

    History & Heritage

    Developed in 1958 by Mannon C. Gore, Sunset Beach was originally accessible only by a historic wooden pontoon swing bridge across the Intracoastal Waterway. Incorporated in 1963, the town has preserved its peaceful barrier island character while expanding thoughtfully onto the mainland.

    Quiet Coastal Sanctuary

    Sunset Beach offers an unhurried, natural lifestyle. Without high-rise commercial towers, days are spent walking to Bird Island, exploring tidal creeks in kayaks, playing rounds of golf at Sea Trail, and gathering at the waterfront park for evening sunset views.

    Why People Visit

    Travelers from around the world make pilgrimages to write in the Kindred Spirit Mailbox, marvel at stars inside Ingram Planetarium, fish off the historic wooden pier, and soak in uncrowded, wide sand dunes along the Atlantic Ocean.

    Why People Live Here

    Residents cherish the pristine natural surroundings, local golf course developments, low property taxes, safety, proximity to Calabash dining and Shallotte shopping, and a warm, active community.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sunset Beach

    Authoritative answers for visitors, residents, business owners, and search assistants.

    Q:What is Sunset Beach, NC known for?

    Sunset Beach is famous for its peaceful, pristine natural environment, the world-renowned Kindred Spirit Mailbox on Bird Island, the state-of-the-art Ingram Planetarium, and its continuous south-facing white sand beach with breathtaking sunsets.

    Q:Where is Sunset Beach located in Brunswick County?

    Sunset Beach is the southernmost barrier island community in Brunswick County, NC, located between Ocean Isle Beach to the east and the South Carolina border / Little River to the west.

    Q:How do I visit the Kindred Spirit Mailbox?

    The Kindred Spirit Mailbox is located on the beach at Bird Island Reserve. Visitors can park at the 40th Street public beach access on Sunset Beach and walk approximately 1.5 miles west along the shoreline.

    Q:Are dogs allowed on the beach at Sunset Beach, NC?

    From Memorial Day through Labor Day, leashed dogs are permitted on the beach strand before 8:30 AM and after 6:00 PM. During the off-season, leashed dogs are welcome on the beach at all times.

    Q:What parking facilities are available for beachgoers?

    Public beach access parking is available at multiple marked access points along Main Street and East/West Bay Drive, as well as at the Sunset Beach Town Park and near the Sunset Beach Pier.

    Q:Is golf cart driving allowed in Sunset Beach?

    Yes, street-legal, registered golf carts equipped with seatbelts, headlights, turn signals, and insurance are permitted on public roads with posted speed limits of 35 mph or lower.

    Q:What is Bird Island Coastal Reserve?

    Bird Island is a 1,300-acre dedicated North Carolina Coastal Reserve consisting of undeveloped barrier island dunes, salt marshes, and tidal creeks adjoining Sunset Beach at the South Carolina state line.

    Q:Where are public boat ramps and kayak launches located?

    Public kayak and paddleboard launches are located at the Sunset Beach Town Park on the Intracoastal Waterway. Motorized boat launches are accessible nearby at the NC Wildlife Ramp under the Ocean Isle bridge.

    Q:Are glass containers, alcohol, or bonfires allowed on the beach?

    No. Glass containers, alcoholic beverages, open fires, and fireworks are strictly prohibited on all public beach strands in Sunset Beach to protect visitors and wildlife.

    Q:What healthcare facilities serve Sunset Beach residents and visitors?

    Novant Health Ocean Isle Emergency Department and Brunswick Novant Medical Center in Supply/Shallotte provide 24/7 emergency and urgent medical care just minutes from Sunset Beach.

    Q:What golf courses are located in or around Sunset Beach?

    Sunset Beach is home to Sea Trail Resort (featuring three 18-hole championship courses: Byrd, Maples, and Jones), Oyster Bay Golf Links, and Sandpiper Bay Golf Country Club.

    Getting To & Around Sunset Beach

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-17 to NC-179 west, then across the Mannon C. Gore high-rise bridge onto the island. ~50 min from Wilmington, ~30 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 35 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 50 miles north.

    Public Parking

    Free public parking at designated beach access points along Main Street and West 40th Street. Limited spaces during peak season.

    Golf Carts

    Street-legal registered golf carts permitted on island roads with speed limits 35 mph or less. Multiple rental vendors on the mainland.

    Boat Access

    Public boat ramp at Twin Lakes area. Kayak launches near the Intracoastal Waterway bridge approach.

    Public Transit

    No fixed-route public transit. Personal vehicle recommended. Rideshare available seasonally.

    Accessibility at Sunset Beach

    Beach Wheelchairs

    Free beach wheelchair loans available through Sunset Beach Town Hall. Contact (910) 579-6297 for reservations.

    Accessible Walkways

    Mobi-mats installed at primary beach access points during summer season. Boardwalk at the main access is ramped.

    Bird Island Access

    The Kindred Spirit Mailbox trail on Bird Island is a natural sand path — not paved. Accessible beach wheelchairs can navigate the firm sand at low tide.

    Accessible Restrooms

    ADA-compliant restrooms available at the Town Park and main beach access parking area.
