    Belville History & Community Overview

    Welcome to Belville, North Carolina

    Belville is an inviting waterfront town located along the picturesque Brunswick River in northern Brunswick County. Incorporated in 1977, Belville has evolved from a quiet rural settlement into a vibrant riverfront enclave that balances natural conservation with smart residential and commercial growth.

    At the heart of the community is the magnificent Brunswick Riverwalk Park. Spanning acres of pristine waterfront and marshland, the park features a 125-foot wooden fishing pier, an ADA-accessible kayak and canoe launch, elevated nature boardwalks, a picnic pavilion, children's playground, and an outdoor amphitheater that hosts seasonal concerts and the annual Belville Riverfest.

    Belville's strategic location makes it one of the most desirable communities in the region. Situated just 5 minutes across the river from downtown Wilmington and bordering the municipality of Leland, residents enjoy immediate access to metropolitan dining, healthcare, arts, and shopping while residing in a tranquil riverfront town.

    Belville FAQ & Local Intelligence

    Frequently Asked Questions About Belville, NC

    Verified answers for residents, visitors, homebuyers, and local businesses

    Q:Where is Belville, North Carolina located?

    Belville is a picturesque waterfront town in northern Brunswick County, NC. It sits along the western banks of the Brunswick River, immediately south of Leland and just 5 minutes across the river from historic downtown Wilmington.

    Q:What is Belville, NC known for?

    Belville is famous for its Brunswick Riverwalk Park—a premier waterfront destination featuring a 125-foot public fishing pier, boardwalks, a kayak launch, picnic pavilions, an outdoor amphitheater, and stunning views of the Brunswick River.

    Q:What facilities are available at Brunswick Riverwalk Park in Belville?

    The park offers a 125-foot wooden fishing pier, an ADA-accessible kayak and canoe launch, elevated marsh boardwalks, picnic shelters with grills, a children’s playground, modern restrooms, a riverfront walking trail, and a farmers market pavilion.

    Q:Can you launch a kayak or boat in Belville, NC?

    Yes, Brunswick Riverwalk Park features a dedicated public kayak launch giving paddlers immediate access to the Brunswick River, surrounding tidal creeks, and the Cape Fear River estuary.

    Q:How far is Belville from downtown Wilmington, NC?

    Belville is located just 5 to 7 miles from downtown Wilmington, allowing residents to reach Wilmington’s historic district, restaurants, and cultural venues in under 10 minutes via US-17 / US-74.

    Q:How far is Belville, NC from Brunswick County beaches?

    Belville is approximately 25 minutes from Oak Island Beach and Caswell Beach, and about 30 to 35 minutes from Wrightsville Beach, Holden Beach, and Ocean Isle Beach.

    Q:What school district serves Belville, NC?

    Belville is served by Brunswick County Schools, including Belville Elementary School, Leland Middle School, and North Brunswick High School. Higher education facilities like Brunswick Community College and UNC Wilmington are within easy driving distance.

    Q:What types of real estate and homes are available in Belville, NC?

    Belville features a diverse selection of housing including riverfront condominiums, established single-family neighborhoods, modern residential developments, and custom homesites with river access.

    Q:What official local resources are located in Belville, NC?

    Official resources include Belville Town Hall (townofbelville.com), Belville Elementary School, North Brunswick Chamber of Commerce resources, Belville Public Works, and the North Brunswick Volunteer Fire Department.

    Q:Are there public events and farmers markets in Belville?

    Yes! Belville hosts a weekly seasonal Farmers Market at the Riverwalk Park Pavilion, outdoor summer concerts at the amphitheater, the annual Belville Riverfest, and seasonal holiday celebrations.

    Q:Is fishing permitted at Brunswick Riverwalk Park?

    Yes, public fishing is permitted off the 125-foot pier. Anglers catch drum, trout, flounder, stripers, and blue crab. A valid North Carolina Coastal Recreational Fishing License (CRFL) is required.

    Getting To & Around Belville

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-17/US-74/76, adjacent to Leland. ~12 min to downtown Wilmington, ~30 min to Oak Island.

    Nearest Airport

    Wilmington International (ILM) — 12 miles east. Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 65 miles south.

    Parking

    Free parking at Belville Town Hall, Belville River Park, and commercial areas along US-17.

    River Access

    Brunswick River access at Belville River Park with boat launch and fishing pier.

    Boat Access

    Brunswick River and Sturgeon Creek provide kayaking and small boat access near the town.

    Public Transit

    Wave Transit provides limited service. Personal vehicle recommended.

    Accessibility at Belville

    Belville River Park

    Fully accessible park with paved walkways, accessible fishing pier, and ADA-compliant restrooms.

    Town Hall

    Belville Town Hall is ADA-compliant with accessible parking and entrance.

    Riverwalk

    Brunswick Riverwalk trail sections near Belville are paved and accessible.

    Commercial Areas

    US-17 commercial centers have accessible parking and ADA-compliant facilities.
