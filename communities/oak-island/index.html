    COMMUNITY PROFILE & HISTORY

    About Oak Island,
    North Carolina

    Incorporated in 1999 through the consolidation of the long-standing beach communities of Long Beach and Yaupon Beach, Oak Island is Brunswick County’s largest barrier island town. Spanning 10 miles of ocean shoreline along the Atlantic, Oak Island is famous for its south-facing beach orientation, peaceful residential neighborhoods, and absence of oceanfront high-rises.

    From the historic Oak Island Lighthouse watching over the Cape Fear River channel to the wooden planks of Ocean Crest Pier and Oak Island Pier, the island balances classic coastal nostalgia with modern family amenities. Residents and visitors enjoy 65+ public beach access points, dog-friendly beaches, salt marsh kayak trails along Davis Canal, golf, and locally owned seafood restaurants.

    Why People Visit

    Pristine 10-mile beach strand, two ocean piers, lighthouse tours, boating, fishing, and pet-friendly vacation rentals.

    Why People Live Here

    Welcoming coastal neighborhood feel, golf cart accessible streets, excellent schools, proximity to Southport, and low density.

    LOCAL KNOWLEDGE & VISITOR ADVISORY

    Frequently Asked Questions About Oak Island

    What makes Oak Island, NC a popular coastal destination?

    Oak Island is renowned for its 10 miles of uncrowded south-facing ocean beaches, two iconic fishing piers, the historic Oak Island Lighthouse, 65+ public beach access points, and a friendly, relaxed island town feel with no high-rise concrete commercial towers.

    Where is Oak Island located in Brunswick County?

    Oak Island is situated in southeastern Brunswick County, NC, bounded by the Atlantic Ocean to the south, the Intracoastal Waterway and Davis Canal to the north, the Cape Fear River & Caswell Beach to the east, and Lockwoods Folly Inlet to the west.

    Can visitors climb the Oak Island Lighthouse?

    Yes. Top-level climb tours to the lantern room of the 153-foot Oak Island Lighthouse are available year-round by advance reservation through the Friends of Oak Island Lighthouse. Docent tours of the base are free during summer months.

    What are the beach rules for dogs on Oak Island?

    Dogs are permitted on Oak Island beaches year-round! From April 1 to September 30, dogs must be kept on a leash between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Outside these hours and during the off-season, dogs may be off-leash in designated areas if under strict voice control.

    How does parking work for Oak Island beach accesses?

    Oak Island uses a seasonal paid parking program (April 1 – September 30) for public access spaces and lots. Residents can register vehicles for passes, and visitors pay via mobile app or designated kiosks.

    Are golf carts legal on Oak Island streets?

    Yes. Street-legal, state-registered golf carts equipped with seatbelts, headlights, tail lights, windshields, and insurance are permitted on public streets with posted speed limits of 35 mph or lower.

    What public boat ramps and kayak launches serve Oak Island?

    Oak Island features multiple public boating facilities including the Dutchman Creek Park NC Wildlife boat ramp, the Blue Water Point Marina, and kayak launches along Davis Canal and the Intracoastal Waterway at 57th Place West and 30th Street Park.

    What fishing piers are available on Oak Island?

    Oak Island features two classic wooden ocean piers: Ocean Crest Pier (1,000 ft long, ideal for king mackerel) and Oak Island Pier (rebuilt, featuring tackle shop and restaurant).

    Where is emergency medical care located near Oak Island?

    Emergency 911 medical response is provided by Oak Island Fire & EMS. Urgent care clinics are located on E. Oak Island Drive and in nearby Southport. Dosher Memorial Hospital in Southport is less than 10 minutes away.

    Are bonfires or glass bottles allowed on Oak Island beaches?

    No. Open fires, bonfires, fireworks, glass containers, and littering are strictly prohibited on all Oak Island oceanfront beaches to ensure public safety and protect coastal wildlife.

    What golf courses are located on or near Oak Island?

    Oak Island Golf Club is located right on the island (18-hole George Cobb design). Nearby courses include Carolina National in Bolivia and the four championship courses at St. James Plantation.

    Getting To & Around Oak Island

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via NC-211 or US-17 to Long Beach Road (NC-133), crossing the G.V. Barbee Sr. Bridge onto the island. ~35 min from Wilmington, ~45 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 50 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 35 miles north.

    Public Parking

    Free public parking at 65+ beach access points along Beach Drive East and West. Additional parking at Cabana Park and town facilities.

    Golf Carts

    Street-legal registered golf carts permitted on roads 35 mph or less. Multiple rental vendors across the island.

    Boat Ramps

    Public boat ramp at the Wildlife Landing on the Intracoastal Waterway. Kayak launches at Cabana Park and Waterway Park.

    Public Transit

    No fixed-route public transit. Personal vehicle recommended. Rideshare and taxi services available seasonally.

    Accessibility at Oak Island

    Beach Wheelchairs

    Free beach wheelchair loans available through Oak Island Parks & Recreation (910-278-5518). Multiple wheelchairs available seasonally.

    Accessible Beach Access

    65+ beach access points, with ADA-compliant ramps and Mobi-mats at several primary accesses along Beach Drive.

    Cabana Park

    Fully accessible park with paved walkways, accessible restrooms, changing facilities, and parking.

    Oak Island Lighthouse

    The lighthouse base area is accessible. The 131-step climb to the top is not wheelchair accessible. Viewing area and gift shop are accessible.
