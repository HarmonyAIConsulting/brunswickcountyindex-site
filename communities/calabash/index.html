    Where Culinary Tradition Meets Coastal Charm

    Nestled along the winding, tidal waters of the Calabash River in southwestern Brunswick County, Calabash is world-famous as the home of light, crispy, cornmeal-dusted fried seafood. What began as a humble fishing village in the early 20th century has grown into a beloved coastal destination celebrated for its maritime heritage, riverfront dining docks, and warm Southern hospitality.

    Beyond its culinary fame, Calabash offers an enviable lifestyle for residents and visitors alike. With championship golf courses winding through coastal pine forests, boutique shopping, marina boat accesses, and immediate proximity to the pristine beaches of Sunset Beach and Ocean Isle Beach, Calabash blends small-town relaxation with world-class amenities.

    Calabash Frequently Asked Questions

    Why is Calabash, NC known as the "Seafood Capital of the World"?

    Calabash earned its legendary title in the 1930s when local fishing families began serving fresh seafood lightly battered in cornmeal and fried to crisp perfection, accompanied by homemade hushpuppies. Today, millions of visitors travel to Calabash annually to experience authentic "Calabash-Style" seafood at waterfront restaurants along the Calabash River.

    Where is Calabash located in Brunswick County?

    Calabash is located in the extreme southwestern corner of Brunswick County, North Carolina, sitting right along the South Carolina state border adjacent to Little River, SC, and just minutes from Sunset Beach and Ocean Isle Beach.

    What is Calabash-style seafood?

    Calabash-style seafood features fresh fish, shrimp, oysters, clams, and scallops dipped in evaporated milk, lightly dredged in seasoned cornmeal flour, and quick-fried in clean oil until golden and delicate. It is served with hushpuppies, coleslaw, and tartar or cocktail sauce.

    What are the top waterfront restaurants in Calabash?

    Famous riverfront seafood spots include Captain Nance's Seafood, Captain John's Seafood, Calabash Seafood Hut, and Waterfront Seafood Shack, where diners can enjoy fresh catches overlooking active shrimp boats.

    How far is Calabash from the beach?

    Calabash is located on the mainland tidal river, just a 5 to 10-minute drive (3 to 5 miles) from the public beach accesses at Sunset Beach and Ocean Isle Beach.

    What is Callahan’s in Calabash?

    Callahan's Nautical Gifts is a famous 35,000-square-foot retail landmark in Calabash featuring massive collections of nautical decor, coastal gifts, clothing, Christmas ornaments, jewelry, and gourmet candies.

    What golf courses are located in or near Calabash?

    Calabash is surrounded by world-class golf courses including The Pearl Golf Links, Oyster Bay Golf Links, Sea Trail Golf Resort, Crow Creek Golf Club, and Meadowlands Golf Club.

    Can you go charter fishing from Calabash?

    Yes! Several charter fishing operations and headboats operate directly out of the Calabash River docks, offering inshore species trips (red drum, flounder, speckled trout) and deep-sea offshore fishing into the Atlantic Ocean.

    What events and festivals take place in Calabash?

    Key annual events include the Calabash Summer Concert Series at Town Park, the Calabash Oktoberfest and Craft Festival in October, and the annual Christmas Tree Lighting and Parade in December.

    Is Calabash a good place to live or retire?

    Calabash is a local coastal retirement and residential destination due to its low tax rates, warm climate, proximity to healthcare, abundant golf courses, fresh seafood, and relaxed maritime lifestyle.

    What river runs through Calabash?

    The Calabash River meanders through the town and connects directly into Little River Inlet, the Intracoastal Waterway, and the Atlantic Ocean.

    Where is the town hall and government center in Calabash?

    Calabash Town Hall is located at 882 Persimmon Road, providing administrative, planning, public safety, and community services for residents and visitors.

    Getting To & Around Calabash

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-17 to NC-179 south, at the NC/SC border. ~1 hr from Wilmington, ~25 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 25 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 60 miles north.

    Parking

    Free parking along the waterfront boardwalk, downtown dining district, and at most restaurants and shops.

    Nearby Beaches

    10-minute drive to Sunset Beach via NC-179. 15 minutes to Ocean Isle Beach.

    Boat Access

    Calabash River marina and boat docks. Charter fishing boats depart from the waterfront daily.

    Public Transit

    No fixed-route public transit. Personal vehicle recommended.

    Accessibility at Calabash

    Waterfront Boardwalk

    The Calabash waterfront boardwalk is accessible with paved surfaces and accessible parking nearby.

    Restaurant Access

    Most waterfront restaurants have ramped entry. Call ahead for specific accessibility details.

    Restrooms

    ADA-compliant public restrooms available near the waterfront dock area.

    Docks & Boardwalk

    Boardwalk viewing of shrimp boats and docks is accessible. Some dock-level access may require steps.
