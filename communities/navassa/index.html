    Heritage & Community

    About Navassa, North Carolina

    Navassa holds a unique and revered place in Brunswick County's story. Incorporated in 1977, the town’s roots stretch back to the 1700s when rice plantations flourished along the nutrient-rich Brunswick and Cape Fear River estuaries.

    As an integral component of the Gullah Geechee Cultural Heritage Corridor, Navassa honors the deep cultural, culinary, and spiritual traditions passed down by African ancestors. Today, community leaders and residents actively preserve these historic roots through story-telling, cultural exhibits, and heritage celebrations.

    In addition to its cultural significance, Navassa plays a crucial role in northern Brunswick County’s economic vitality. Positioned along major rail lines, industrial shipping corridors, and Sturgeon Creek water access, Navassa offers a rare combination of industrial growth potential and peaceful riverfront neighborhood living.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Navassa, NC

    Answers to common queries regarding Navassa heritage, water access, real estate, and resources

    Where is Navassa, North Carolina located?

    Navassa is a historic riverfront town in northern Brunswick County, NC. It is situated along the western banks of the Brunswick River and Sturgeon Creek, just north of Leland and Belville, and approximately 10 minutes from downtown Wilmington.

    What is Navassa, NC famous for?

    Navassa is renowned for its rich Gullah Geechee cultural heritage, deep historic roots tied to 18th-century rice plantations and river commerce, its location along the Brunswick River, and its key role in northern Brunswick County’s economic development.

    What is the Gullah Geechee connection to Navassa, NC?

    Navassa is an officially recognized community within the federally designated Gullah Geechee Cultural Heritage Corridor. Descendants of enslaved West Africans who worked rice plantations along the river have preserved traditions, foodways, language, and craftsmanship here for generations.

    What water access is available in Navassa, NC?

    Navassa provides water access via Sturgeon Creek Park and river launches. Boat owners, kayakers, and anglers can launch into Sturgeon Creek to reach the main channel of the Brunswick River and Cape Fear River estuary.

    How far is Navassa from downtown Wilmington, NC?

    Navassa is located approximately 6 to 8 miles from downtown Wilmington. Residents can easily commute across the Cape Fear River bridges via US-17 / US-74 in under 12 minutes.

    How far is Navassa, NC from Brunswick County beaches?

    Navassa is about 25 to 30 minutes from Oak Island Beach and Caswell Beach, and approximately 35 minutes from Wrightsville Beach, Holden Beach, and Ocean Isle Beach.

    What school district serves Navassa, NC?

    Navassa is served by Brunswick County Schools, including Lincoln Elementary School, Leland Middle School, and North Brunswick High School. Higher education institutions like Brunswick Community College and UNC Wilmington are located nearby.

    What economic growth and development is happening in Navassa, NC?

    Navassa is experiencing steady growth with industrial rail access, commercial expansion, riverfront environmental restoration, and new residential neighborhood proposals catering to northern Brunswick County’s expanding workforce.

    What official local resources are located in Navassa, NC?

    Official resources include Navassa Town Hall (townofnavassa.org), the Navassa Community Center, Sturgeon Creek Park, and North Brunswick Fire & Rescue Station.

    What recreational opportunities are available in Navassa, NC?

    Recreation includes boating, kayak paddling along Sturgeon Creek, fishing on the Brunswick River, nature photography, bird watching, community sports, and walking along scenic waterfront trails.

    What types of real estate are available in Navassa, NC?

    Real estate options include affordable single-family homes, historic family parcels, riverfront land, new residential subdivisions, and commercial/industrial development parcels.

    How can businesses list on Brunswick County Index for Navassa, NC?

    Local business owners and service providers in Navassa can submit or claim their listing by clicking "Claim Your Business" or "Submit A Business" on the Brunswick County Index portal.

    Getting To & Around Navassa

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-74/76 west of Wilmington. ~15 min to downtown Wilmington, ~35 min to Oak Island.

    Nearest Airport

    Wilmington International (ILM) — 15 miles east.

    Parking

    Free parking at Navassa Town Hall and community areas.

    River Access

    Located at the confluence of the Navassa and Brunswick Rivers. Historic rice plantation waterfront.

    Boat Access

    Brunswick River access for kayaking and small boats. Public landing near town center.

    Public Transit

    Limited Wave Transit service. Personal vehicle recommended.

    Accessibility at Navassa

    Town Hall

    Navassa Town Hall is accessible with ramped entry and accessible parking.

    Community Center

    The community center provides accessible meeting space and restrooms.

    River Access

    Brunswick River access points have natural surfaces. Accessible viewing from parking areas.

    Historic Sites

    Some historic plantation sites have unpaved paths. Contact the town for accessibility information.
