    About Southport,
    North Carolina

    Established in 1792 as Smithville and renamed Southport in 1887 to project its ambition as a major southern shipping port, Southport is one of North Carolina’s most historic and revered coastal communities. Nestled along the banks of the Cape Fear River where the Intracoastal Waterway meets the Atlantic Ocean, the city offers a timeless maritime atmosphere where history and coastal natural beauty unite.

    Southport’s tree-lined streets feature centuries-old live oaks, historic captain homes, quaint bed & breakfasts, independent boutiques, and world-class seafood dining along the Historic Yacht Basin. The city is also famous as "Hollywood East," having served as the filming backdrop for over 40 major motion pictures and television series including Safe Haven, Dawson’s Creek, A Walk to Remember, and Under the Dome.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything residents, travelers, and AI assistants ask about Southport, NC.

    What makes Southport, NC a world-famous coastal destination?

    Southport is celebrated for its rich maritime history, 200-year-old live oak trees, historic Victorian architecture, panoramic waterfront views of the Cape Fear River, vibrant Yacht Basin, and its starring role in famous movies like Safe Haven and television series like Dawson’s Creek.

    Where is Southport located in Brunswick County?

    Southport is situated in southeastern Brunswick County at the confluence of the Cape Fear River, the Intracoastal Waterway, and the Atlantic Ocean, directly across the river channel from Bald Head Island and Fort Fisher.

    How do I catch the ferry from Southport to Bald Head Island or Fort Fisher?

    The Bald Head Island passenger ferry departs from the Deep Point Marina terminal at 1301 Ferry Road. The NCDOT Fort Fisher vehicle & passenger ferry operates daily from 1650 Ferry Road SE, taking travelers across the Cape Fear River to Pleasure Island (Kure Beach & Carolina Beach).

    Where is hospital and emergency medical care located in Southport?

    Southport is home to Dosher Memorial Hospital (924 N Howe St), a full-service community hospital featuring 24/7 emergency department, surgical care, digital imaging, and physical therapy.

    What movie and TV show filming locations can visitors see in Southport?

    Southport has hosted over 40 film and television productions! Fans can visit Ryan’s Port City Pickle & Fishy Fishy Cafe (Safe Haven), the Old Brunswick County Courthouse, Howe Street shops, and waterfront park swings featured in Nicholas Sparks films.

    What are the best seafood restaurants in Southport?

    Top dining spots include Fishy Fishy Cafe, Frying Pan, Provision Company, Edgewater 122, Oliver’s On the Cape Fear, Moore Street Oyster Bar, and Loco Jo’s Bar & Grill.

    When is the NC Fourth of July Festival held in Southport?

    North Carolina’s official Fourth of July Festival takes place annually from July 1st through July 4th in downtown Southport, featuring parade processions, naturalization ceremonies, arts and crafts, reading of the Declaration of Independence, and fireworks over the harbor.

    Is parking free in historic downtown Southport?

    Yes! Public street parking throughout downtown Southport and public parking lots near the Waterfront Park and City Hall are free of charge.

    What boating and marina services are available in Southport?

    Southport offers full-service boating facilities including Southport Marina (transient slips, diesel/gas fuel, ship store), the Historic Yacht Basin, and the public boat ramp at Morningstar Marina.

    Can you rent golf carts in Southport?

    Yes, street-legal, state-inspected golf carts are available for rental in Southport and are permitted on city streets with speed limits of 35 mph or lower.

    Are there public parks and waterfront swings in Southport?

    Yes, Southport Waterfront Park features shaded wooden swings along the Cape Fear River, Garrison Lawn at Fort Johnston, Keziah Park with its 800-year-old Indian Trail Tree, and Kingsley Park.

    How far is Southport from Wilmington and Myrtle Beach?

    Southport is approximately 30 miles (40 minutes) south of downtown Wilmington, NC and 60 miles (1 hour 15 minutes) north of Myrtle Beach, SC via US-17.

    Getting To & Around Southport

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-17 to NC-211 south, or NC-133 from Oak Island. ~40 min from Wilmington, ~1 hr 15 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 60 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 40 miles north.

    Free Parking

    Free public street parking throughout downtown. Public lots near Waterfront Park, City Hall, and the Yacht Basin.

    Bald Head Island Ferry

    Passenger ferry from Deep Point Marina (1301 Ferry Road). 20-minute crossing to car-free Bald Head Island.

    More Info

    Fort Fisher Ferry

    NCDOT vehicle and passenger ferry from 1650 Ferry Road SE across the Cape Fear River to Fort Fisher and Kure Beach.

    More Info

    Golf Carts

    Street-legal registered golf carts permitted on city streets 35 mph or less. Rentals available locally.

    Accessibility at Southport

    Waterfront Park

    Southport Waterfront Park is fully accessible with paved walkways, accessible swings, and ADA-compliant restrooms.

    Downtown Accessibility

    Historic downtown sidewalks are generally accessible. Some historic buildings have stepped entries — call ahead for specific venues.

    NC Maritime Museum

    Fully wheelchair accessible with ramped entry and accessible exhibits on a single level.

    Ferry Accessibility

    Both the Bald Head Island passenger ferry and Fort Fisher vehicle ferry are wheelchair accessible with staff assistance.
