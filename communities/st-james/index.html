    About St. James, North Carolina

    Incorporated as a town in 1999, St. James is a master-planned, gated community designed around the natural beauty of the North Carolina coast. Spanning thousands of pristine acres between the Intracoastal Waterway and pine forests, St. James has earned national acclaim as one of America’s top resort and retirement destinations.

    Life in St. James centers on health, camaraderie, and outdoor recreation. Residents enjoy 81 holes of championship golf across four distinct clubhouses, an active racquet sports community, four state-of-the-art wellness centers, and a 155-slip full-service marina. With a private Beach Club located on nearby Oak Island, residents experience true oceanfront luxury paired with security and peace of mind.

    Local Knowledge

    St. James, NC Frequently Asked Questions

    Detailed answers about St. James real estate, amenities, golf, and coastal living.

    Where is St. James, North Carolina located?

    St. James, NC is an incorporated town situated along the Intracoastal Waterway in eastern Brunswick County, just 5 miles from historic Southport and 7 miles from the beaches of Oak Island. It is approximately 30 miles south of Wilmington, NC and 45 miles north of Myrtle Beach, SC.

    Is St. James, NC a gated community?

    Yes, St. James is a gated, master-planned residential town featuring 24/7 security control at all entrances, ensuring safety, privacy, and peace of mind for residents and guests.

    How many golf courses are in St. James, NC?

    St. James features 81 holes of championship golf across four distinct courses designed by renowned golf architects: Nicklaus Design (The Reserve), P.B. Dye (The Founders), Arthur Hills (The Members), and Hale Irwin (The Players).

    Does St. James have private beach access?

    Yes! St. James residents enjoy exclusive access to the private St. James Beach Club located directly on the oceanfront at Oak Island. Amenities include an oceanfront swimming pool, private showers, restrooms, boardwalk beach access, and outdoor seating.

    What marina facilities are available at St. James?

    The St. James Marina is a full-service marina located on the Intracoastal Waterway. It features 155 wet slips, dry stack storage for over 300 boats, a full fuel dock, boat maintenance services, kayak launches, and the popular Tommy Thompson’s Grille.

    What amenities exist in St. James besides golf and boating?

    St. James boasts 4 clubhouses, 4 fitness facilities, indoor lap pools, outdoor resort-style pools, 13 tennis courts, pickleball courts, over 30 miles of walking/biking trails, dog parks, a community garden, a kayak launch, and over 100 social and volunteer clubs.

    What real estate options are available in St. James, NC?

    Real estate in St. James ranges from custom single-family luxury homes, fairway estates, and waterfront properties to low-maintenance villas, townhomes, and condominiums overlooking golf courses or nature reserves.

    Is St. James a good place to retire?

    St. James is consistently ranked among the top resort and retirement communities in the United States by Where to Retire magazine. Its active lifestyle, moderate coastal climate, mild winters, extensive healthcare proximity, and rich social calendar make it ideal for active retirees.

    What emergency services serve St. James, NC?

    St. James is served by the St. James Fire Department & EMS, the Brunswick County Sheriff’s Office, and is located just minutes from the Novant Health Southport Emergency Room and the main Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center in Supply/Shallotte.

    What shopping and grocery options exist near St. James?

    Residents have convenient access to nearby supermarkets including Publix, Food Lion, and Lowe’s Foods in Southport and Oak Island, as well as local boutique shops, hardware stores, and medical offices within a 5-to-10-minute drive.

    Are pets allowed in St. James, NC?

    Yes, St. James is a very pet-friendly community featuring miles of paved walking trails, dedicated dog parks (including Waterway Park Dog Park), and pet-welcoming outdoor spaces.

    How do I learn more about homes or visiting St. James?

    You can explore real estate listings, community amenities, local businesses, and visitor guides right here on Brunswick County Index or visit the official Town of St. James and Property Owners Association websites.

    Getting To & Around St. James

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via NC-211 to St. James Drive, west of Southport. ~40 min from Wilmington, ~1 hr from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 55 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 40 miles north.

    Parking

    Private parking within the gated community. Guest parking at the St. James Marina and clubhouse facilities.

    Golf Carts

    Golf carts are the primary mode of transportation within the gated community. Extensive cart path network throughout.

    St. James Marina

    Full-service marina on the Intracoastal Waterway with transient slips, fuel, and boat storage.

    Public Transit

    No public transit. Personal vehicle or golf cart recommended within the community.

    Accessibility at St. James

    Marina Accessibility

    St. James Marina is accessible with paved walkways, accessible parking, and ADA-compliant restrooms.

    Clubhouse Access

    All clubhouse and dining facilities are ADA-compliant with accessible entries and restrooms.

    Golf Cart Access

    Golf carts provide mobility for all residents and visitors throughout the community path network.

    Beach Access

    Private beach access points have ramped entry. Beach wheelchairs available through member services.
