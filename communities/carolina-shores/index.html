    What Makes Carolina Shores, NC Unique?

    Carolina Shores, NC (ZIP 28467) is an incorporated residential town located in southwestern Brunswick County, North Carolina, positioned directly adjacent to the seafood village of Calabash and minutes from Sunset Beach. Incorporated in 1998, Carolina Shores is widely renowned for its lush tree-canopied residential subdivisions, active retirement lifestyle, low property taxes, and Carolina Shores Golf & Country Club.

    Its ideal geography along Highway 17 and NC-179 puts residents less than 10 minutes from barrier island oceanfront beaches, world-famous Calabash seafood dining, and the entertainment attractions of North Myrtle Beach, South Carolina.

    About Carolina Shores, North Carolina

    A Vibrant Golf & Residential Sanctuary

    Originally planned around the Carolina Shores Golf & Country Club in the 1970s and officially incorporated as a town in 1998, Carolina Shores has grown into one of Brunswick County’s most desirable and well-maintained residential destinations.

    The town offers quiet, tree-shaded streets lined with single-family custom homes, villas, patio homes, and pristine golf course properties. Residents enjoy a peaceful, friendly atmosphere with low municipal taxes, well-maintained parks, pickleball courts, and active neighborhood associations.

    Local Knowledge

    Carolina Shores Frequently Asked Questions

    Common questions about living, visiting, retiring, and doing business in Carolina Shores, NC.

    Where is Carolina Shores, North Carolina located?

    Carolina Shores is an incorporated residential town located in southwestern Brunswick County, NC. It directly borders Calabash, NC to the east and Little River, SC to the south, sitting just minutes inland from Sunset Beach.

    What is Carolina Shores, NC best known for?

    Carolina Shores is best known as a premier golf and residential town featuring Carolina Shores Golf & Country Club, tree-shaded neighborhoods, active retirement communities, low tax rates, and unmatched proximity to both beaches and North Myrtle Beach.

    What type of real estate is available in Carolina Shores, NC?

    Real estate in Carolina Shores features custom single-family homes, golf course residences, low-maintenance townhomes, patio homes, and condominium communities. Many neighborhoods offer tree-lined streets, generous lot sizes, and active HOAs.

    How far is Carolina Shores, NC from the beach?

    Carolina Shores is located just 4 to 6 miles (about a 5-to-10 minute drive) from Sunset Beach and Ocean Isle Beach, allowing residents to enjoy coastal beach outings with quiet inland living.

    How far is Carolina Shores, NC from North Myrtle Beach, SC?

    Carolina Shores sits right on the North Carolina / South Carolina border, just 5 to 10 minutes from Little River and North Myrtle Beach via Highway 17 or SC-9.

    Is Carolina Shores a good place to retire?

    Yes, Carolina Shores is widely considered one of the top retirement communities in Brunswick County due to its active lifestyle, mild coastal climate, golf amenities, proximity to quality healthcare, and peaceful neighborhood setting.

    What golf amenities are located in Carolina Shores?

    The town is home to Carolina Shores Golf & Country Club, an 18-hole championship course designed by Tom Jackson. Additionally, dozens of top-rated Grand Strand and Brunswick County golf courses are located within a 10-minute drive.

    What public schools and educational resources serve Carolina Shores?

    Children in Carolina Shores attend Brunswick County Public Schools, including Jessie Mae Monroe Elementary, Shallotte Middle School, and West Brunswick High School, alongside nearby private and charter school options.

    What local government services serve Carolina Shores, NC?

    Carolina Shores is an incorporated town with its own Town Hall, Mayor, Town Council, and Planning Board. Public safety is supported by local law enforcement, emergency medical services, and nearby volunteer fire departments.

    Are there parks and outdoor trails in Carolina Shores?

    Yes, Carolina Shores maintains town park facilities, tennis and pickleball courts, walking paths, and beautiful open green spaces for residents and visitors.

    What dining and shopping options are near Carolina Shores?

    Residents enjoy local diners, bakeries, and cafes in Carolina Shores, as well as world-famous Calabash seafood restaurants just 1 minute away and major retail shopping in nearby Shallotte and North Myrtle Beach.

    Getting To & Around Carolina Shores

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-17 at the NC/SC border, west of Calabash. ~1 hr from Wilmington, ~20 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 20 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 65 miles north.

    Parking

    Free parking at the Carolina Shores clubhouse, golf course, and community areas.

    Gated Community

    Carolina Shores is a gated golf community. Guest access via the main gate on US-17 Business.

    Boat Access

    Nearby Calabash River and Tubbs Inlet provide kayaking and fishing access.

    Public Transit

    No public transit. Personal vehicle recommended.

    Accessibility at Carolina Shores

    Clubhouse Access

    Carolina Shores clubhouse and golf facilities are ADA-compliant with accessible entries and restrooms.

    Golf Cart Access

    Golf cart paths throughout the community provide mobility for all residents and visitors.

    Community Center

    Community center facilities are accessible with paved parking and ramped entry.

    Nearby Services

    Full ADA-compliant services available in nearby Calabash and Myrtle Beach.
