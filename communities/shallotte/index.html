    AI OVERVIEW — SHALLOTTE AT A GLANCE

    What Makes Shallotte, NC the Central Hub of South Brunswick County?

    Shallotte, NC is the principal commercial, financial, and healthcare capital of South Brunswick County. Centrally located at the intersection of US-17 and NC-130 along the picturesque Shallotte River, Shallotte provides modern medical infrastructure (Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center), regional shopping districts, professional services, and high-performing schools for over 40,000 year-round residents and millions of annual coastal visitors. Just 10 minutes from Ocean Isle Beach, Holden Beach, and Sunset Beach, Shallotte blends inland riverfront charm with bustling trade and modern residential communities.

    HERITAGE & GROWTH

    From Historic River Port to Modern Regional Capital

    Incorporated in 1899 along the banks of the Shallotte River, Shallotte originally developed as a vital river trading post where flatboats and schooners carried timber, naval stores, and agricultural goods out to the Atlantic Ocean. The town’s name is derived from early colonial river references, honoring its historic waterway connection.

    Today, Shallotte has transformed into the premier economic motor of southern Brunswick County. While maintaining its friendly small-town hospitality, Shallotte provides state-of-the-art medical complexes, modern shopping centers, corporate headquarters, and vibrant municipal parks.

    Why People Live & Invest in Shallotte

    • Unmatched Convenience: Minutes from Ocean Isle, Sunset Beach, and Holden Beach without high oceanfront real estate prices.
    • Top-Tier Medical Access: Direct proximity to Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center and comprehensive specialist offices.
    • Thriving Commerce: Full array of national retailers, local boutiques, banks, law firms, and home contractors.

    LOCAL VISITOR & RESIDENT FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shallotte, NC

    Why is Shallotte considered the commercial and healthcare hub of Brunswick County?

    Shallotte sits geographically in the center of South Brunswick County at the junction of US Highway 17, NC Highway 130, and NC Highway 179. It serves as the primary retail, dining, financial, and medical center for over 40,000 regional residents and seasonal beach visitors from Ocean Isle Beach, Holden Beach, Sunset Beach, and Calabash.

    Where is Shallotte located in North Carolina?

    Shallotte is located in southern Brunswick County along the Shallotte River, approximately 35 miles south of Wilmington, NC, and 30 miles north of Myrtle Beach, SC.

    What healthcare services are available in Shallotte?

    Shallotte is home to Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center, a full-service acute care hospital with a 24/7 Emergency Department, surgical suites, maternity, cardiovascular care, imaging, and extensive primary and specialty care medical offices.

    How far is Shallotte from the nearest beach?

    Shallotte is centrally located just 10 to 15 minutes away from three major barrier island beaches: Ocean Isle Beach (10 miles), Holden Beach (12 miles), and Sunset Beach (14 miles).

    What is Mulberry Street Park in Shallotte?

    Mulberry Street Park is Shallotte’s flagship 12-acre municipal park featuring a riverwalk along the Shallotte River, an outdoor amphitheater for concerts, an interactive splash pad, picnic pavilions, basketball courts, and a community garden.

    What shopping and retail options exist in Shallotte?

    Shallotte offers the largest concentration of shopping in south Brunswick County, including major retailers (Home Depot, Lowe’s, Walmart, Belk, Publix, Harris Teeter) alongside local boutiques, antique stores, and farm markets.

    Are there public boat ramps or river accesses in Shallotte?

    Yes, public boat ramps and kayak launches are located along the Shallotte River, providing direct boat access to the Intracoastal Waterway and Shallotte Inlet.

    What golf courses are in Shallotte?

    Shallotte features Rivers Edge Golf Club, an acclaimed Arnold Palmer Signature course with breathtaking bluff holes along the Shallotte River. Nearby courses include Brierwood Golf Club, Carolina National, and Sea Trail Golf Resort.

    What schools serve the Shallotte community?

    Shallotte is served by Brunswick County Public Schools, including Shallotte Middle School, West Brunswick High School, Union Elementary, and Supply Elementary, as well as charter and private academies.

    Is Shallotte a good place for businesses and retirees?

    Yes! Shallotte is one of the fastest-growing towns in coastal North Carolina, offering top-tier medical infrastructure, central access to beaches, low property taxes, vibrant retail, and strong economic growth.

    What annual events take place in Shallotte?

    Shallotte hosts the Summer Concert Series at Mulberry Park, weekly Farmers Markets from May through October, Movies in the Park, the Shallotte Christmas Parade, and seasonal holiday festivals.

    Getting To & Around Shallotte

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    At the intersection of US-17 and NC-130 — the commercial hub of South Brunswick County. ~35 min from Wilmington, ~30 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 35 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 40 miles north.

    Parking

    Free parking at Mulberry Street Park, Town Centre shopping areas, and along Main Street. Ample lot parking at most retail centers.

    Nearby Beaches

    10-15 minute drive to Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, and Holden Beach via NC-179 or NC-130.

    Boat Access

    Public boat ramp on the Shallotte River off Mulberry Street. Kayak access at the Shallotte Riverwalk at Mulberry Park.

    Public Transit

    No fixed-route public transit. Personal vehicle recommended. Rideshare available.

    Accessibility at Shallotte

    Mulberry Park

    Mulberry Street Park is fully accessible with paved riverwalk trails, accessible playground equipment, and ADA-compliant restrooms.

    Healthcare Access

    Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center is fully ADA-compliant with accessible parking, entrances, and patient facilities.

    Shopping Centers

    Shallotte Town Centre and all major retail centers have accessible parking, ramps, and ADA-compliant facilities.

    Rivers Edge Golf Club

    Accessible clubhouse and dining facilities. Cart paths accommodate mobility devices with advance notice.
