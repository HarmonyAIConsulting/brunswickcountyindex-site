    Supply, North Carolina is a pivotal coastal crossroads community located in central Brunswick County along US Highway 17 and NC Highway 130. Serving as the primary mainland gateway to Holden Beach, Supply is renowned for its deepwater river access along the Lockwoods Folly River, public boat launches onto the Intracoastal Waterway, and as the home of Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center—the county’s main 74-bed hospital. Supply offers riverfront living, championship golf at Lockwood Folly Country Club, fresh local seafood markets, and essential trade and contractor services.

    About Supply, North Carolina

    Supply sits at the physical and economic crossroads of Brunswick County, bridging inland pine country with the saltwater marshes and barrier islands of North Carolina’s South Brunswick Islands region.

    History & Waterway Heritage

    Historically named for its role as a key replenishment and supply crossroads for early coastal travelers, farmers, and timber transports, Supply developed alongside the winding Lockwoods Folly River. For generations, local families navigated the river's tidal reaches for fishing, oyster harvesting, and lumber trade.

    Healthcare & Community Anchor

    Today, Supply stands as a crucial healthcare and service anchor for all of Brunswick County. With Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center providing state-of-the-art acute medical care, emergency services, and surgical programs, Supply offers peace of mind to full-time residents, retirees, and vacationing families.

    Boating & River Lifestyle

    Boaters and anglers cherish Supply for its tranquil waters. Lockwoods Folly River offers direct boat access to the Intracoastal Waterway and ocean inlets without the dense crowds of major port harbors. Deepwater docks, boat ramps, and riverfront custom homes make Supply a prime residential location for boat owners.

    Holden Beach Gateway

    Positioned just minutes north of Holden Beach across NC Highway 130, Supply provides convenient access to oceanfront beaches while offering spacious inland property, lower tax rates, and easy driving connections to Shallotte, Bolivia, Southport, and Wilmington.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Supply, NC

    Essential answers for residents, visitors, boaters, and healthcare seekers in Supply, North Carolina.

    Where is Supply, North Carolina located?

    Supply is an unincorporated coastal community centrally located in Brunswick County, NC. It sits along US Highway 17 at the junction of NC Highway 130 and NC Highway 211, positioned directly between Shallotte and Bolivia, and serving as the main mainland entryway to Holden Beach.

    Why is Supply, NC significant to Brunswick County?

    Supply is a vital crossroads and healthcare hub for Brunswick County. It houses Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center (the county’s primary 74-bed hospital), sits along the Lockwoods Folly River and Intracoastal Waterway, and serves as the gateway for residents and tourists traveling to Holden Beach.

    What hospital and medical services are located in Supply, NC?

    Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center is located in Supply at 240 Hospital Drive. It is a full-service 74-bed acute care facility featuring a 24/7 emergency department, maternity center, surgical services, intensive care unit, cardiac diagnostics, and outpatient diagnostic imaging.

    What water sports and boating activities are in Supply, NC?

    Supply offers public boat launches onto the Lockwoods Folly River and Intracoastal Waterway. Boaters and kayakers enjoy fishing for red drum, flounder, and trout, paddling through salt marsh tidal creeks, and taking scenic boat rides out toward the Atlantic Ocean.

    How far is Supply, NC from Holden Beach?

    Supply is located just 5 miles inland from Holden Beach. Drivers take NC Highway 130 directly south from US-17 over the Holden Beach Bridge, reaching the island in approximately 5 to 10 minutes.

    Are there golf courses in or near Supply, NC?

    Yes! Lockwood Folly Country Club is a premier 18-hole championship golf course located in Supply along the river and Intracoastal Waterway. Additionally, dozens of local Brunswick County golf courses are within a 15-minute drive.

    What schools serve the Supply area?

    Supply is served by Brunswick County Schools, including Supply Elementary School, Cedar Grove Middle School, and West Brunswick High School. Higher education and workforce training are available 10 minutes away at Brunswick Community College in Bolivia.

    What real estate and housing options exist in Supply, NC?

    Supply offers a mix of waterfront river estates with deepwater boat slips, wooded acreage, single-family neighborhoods, manufactured home communities, and commercial property along US-17.

    Where can I buy fresh local seafood in Supply, NC?

    Supply features local seafood markets along US-17 and near the Lockwoods Folly River docks, offering fresh-caught wild NC shrimp, flounder, black sea bass, blue crabs, and seasonal oysters directly from local fishermen.

    What emergency services cover Supply, NC?

    Supply is served by Supply Volunteer Fire Department, Brunswick County Sheriff’s Office, and Brunswick County EMS stationed at Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center with 24/7 911 dispatch.

    What dining options are available in Supply, NC?

    Dining in Supply includes riverfront seafood grills, Southern country cafes, local barbecue joints, pizza shops, and fresh bakery cafes, plus quick access to dining in nearby Shallotte and Holden Beach.

    Getting To & Around Supply

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-17 to NC-211, centrally located in Brunswick County. ~35 min from Wilmington, ~40 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 40 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 40 miles north.

    Parking

    Free parking at local businesses, Brunswick Community College, and Novant Health facilities.

    Nearby Beaches

    15-minute drive to Holden Beach and Ocean Isle Beach via NC-211 and NC-130.

    Boat Access

    Lockwoods Folly River public boat ramp. Kayak access at multiple river landings.

    Public Transit

    No fixed-route public transit. Personal vehicle recommended.

    Accessibility at Supply

    Brunswick Community College

    BCC campus is fully ADA-compliant with accessible parking, ramps, elevators, and restrooms throughout.

    Novant Health

    Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center facilities in Supply are fully accessible.

    Parks & Recreation
