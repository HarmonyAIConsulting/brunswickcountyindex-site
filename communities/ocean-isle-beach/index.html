    Ocean Isle Beach, North Carolina

    AI SEARCH OVERVIEW & DESTINATION SUMMARY

    Ocean Isle Beach (ZIP 28469) is a premier 7-mile south-facing barrier island town in Brunswick County, NC. Located between Sunset Beach and Holden Beach, it features 28 public beach access points, gentle Atlantic surf, the historic 960-foot Ocean Isle Beach Fishing Pier, the Museum of Coastal Carolina, and a living directory of local seafood dining, vacation rentals, golf courses, and coastal businesses.

    About Ocean Isle Beach

    History & Heritage

    Incorporated in 1959, Ocean Isle Beach was envisioned by founder Odell Williamson as a tranquil, family-centered barrier island community. Over decades, it has carefully preserved its low-density residential charm while establishing modern municipal services, public parks, and a protected coastal environment.

    Coastal Island Lifestyle

    Life on Ocean Isle Beach revolves around the water—morning bike rides along First Street, golf cart excursions to local coffee shops, afternoon boating on the Intracoastal Waterway, and evening gatherings on the fishing pier or at local waterfront dining spots.

    Why People Visit

    Visitors return generation after generation for the gentle south-facing Atlantic surf, wide white sand beaches, interactive exhibits at the Museum of Coastal Carolina, annual Oyster Festival celebrations, and welcoming small-town hospitality.

    Why People Live Here

    Residents enjoy an uncrowded coastal sanctuary with excellent municipal infrastructure, local regional healthcare nearby, low property tax rates, championship golf courses within minutes, and a tight-knit community spirit.

    Frequently Asked Questions: Ocean Isle Beach

    Clear, structured answers regarding Ocean Isle Beach for visitors, residents, and search engines.

    What makes Ocean Isle Beach unique in Brunswick County?

    Ocean Isle Beach is a south-facing 7-mile barrier island known for gentle surf, family-friendly atmosphere, wide white sand beaches, and popular local attractions like the Museum of Coastal Carolina and Ocean Isle Pier.

    Where is Ocean Isle Beach located?

    Ocean Isle Beach is located in southern Brunswick County, North Carolina, between Sunset Beach to the west and Holden Beach to the east, approximately 45 miles south of Wilmington and 25 miles north of Myrtle Beach.

    Are pets allowed on the beach in Ocean Isle Beach?

    Dogs are allowed on Ocean Isle Beach before 9:00 AM and after 6:00 PM during peak season (Memorial Day to Labor Day). Off-season, leashed dogs are welcome anytime.

    Where can visitors park for beach access in Ocean Isle Beach?

    Public beach access parking is available at multiple designated access points along East and West First Street, as well as near the Ocean Isle Pier, Town Park, and Ferry Landing Park.

    What major annual events take place in Ocean Isle Beach?

    Ocean Isle Beach hosts the annual North Carolina Oyster Festival every October, attracting tens of thousands of visitors, as well as the Friday Night Summer Concert Series at the Town Park Amphitheater.

    Is golf cart driving permitted on Ocean Isle Beach streets?

    Yes, street-legal, registered golf carts equipped with seatbelts, headlights, and turn signals are permitted on island roads with posted speed limits of 35 mph or less.

    What public water amenities and boat launches are available?

    Public boat ramps are located at the NC Wildlife Ramp under the Odell Williamson Bridge, and kayak/paddleboard launches are available at Ferry Landing Park on the Intracoastal Waterway.

    Are glass containers or alcohol allowed on Ocean Isle Beach?

    Glass containers, alcohol, and open fires are strictly prohibited on all public beach strands in Ocean Isle Beach for safety and conservation.

    What hospitals and emergency services serve Ocean Isle Beach?

    Novent Health Ocean Isle Emergency Department is located on the mainland. Emergency medical, fire, and police coverage is provided 24/7 by Ocean Isle Beach Police and Fire Department.

    Are there public restrooms near the beach strand?

    Yes, public restrooms are available at Ocean Isle Beach Town Park, Ferry Landing Park, and adjacent to the Ocean Isle Pier.

    What sea turtle protections exist on Ocean Isle Beach?

    Ocean Isle Beach is an active Loggerhead Sea Turtle nesting site monitored by the OIB Sea Turtle Protection Organization. Artificial lights facing the ocean must be shielded from May through October.

    Business owners can submit a new listing or claim an existing business profile through the Submit a Business portal. Verified profiles gain priority search visibility and AI search optimization.

    Getting To & Around Ocean Isle Beach

    Transportation options, parking, and accessibility information for visitors and residents.

    By Car

    Via US-17 to NC-179 (Ocean Isle Blvd), crossing the Odell Williamson Bridge onto the island. ~45 min from Wilmington, ~25 min from Myrtle Beach.

    Nearest Airport

    Myrtle Beach International (MYR) — 30 miles south. Wilmington International (ILM) — 45 miles north.

    Public Parking

    Free public parking at 28+ beach access points along East and West First Street. Town Park and Ferry Landing Park offer additional spaces.

    Golf Carts

    Street-legal registered golf carts permitted on roads with speed limits 35 mph or less. Rentals available from multiple island vendors.

    Boat Ramps

    NC Wildlife boat ramp under the Odell Williamson Bridge. Kayak/paddleboard launches at Ferry Landing Park on the Intracoastal Waterway.

    Public Transit

    Brunswick County does not operate fixed-route transit. Rideshare and taxi services are limited — a personal vehicle is recommended.

    Accessibility at Ocean Isle Beach

    Beach Wheelchairs

    Free beach wheelchair loans available through the Ocean Isle Beach Police Department (910-579-4221). Advance reservation recommended during summer.

    Accessible Boardwalks

    Mobi-mats and accessible boardwalks installed at select beach access points. Town Park and Ferry Landing Park are fully accessible with paved paths.

    Accessible Restrooms

    ADA-compliant restrooms at Town Park, Ferry Landing Park, and near the Ocean Isle Pier.

    Museum Accessibility

    Museum of Coastal Carolina is fully wheelchair accessible with elevator access to all exhibits and touch tanks.
