    Brunswick County Real Estate Market Summary

    Brunswick County is North Carolina's fastest-growing coastal county, bridging Wilmington and Myrtle Beach. Offering 45+ miles of barrier island beaches, ICW deep-water channels, and 30+ championship golf courses, property ranges from entry-level single-family subdivisions in Leland to luxury barrier island estates in Ocean Isle Beach and Bald Head Island.

    Brunswick Real Estate FAQs

    Answers to essential buyer, seller, and investor questions curated by Brunswick County real estate specialists.

    Why is Brunswick County, NC one of the fastest-growing real estate markets in the US?

    Brunswick County consistently ranks among the top growth counties in North Carolina and the United States due to its combination of affordable coastal living, mild sub-tropical climate, proximity to Wilmington and Myrtle Beach, low property tax rates, and diverse inventory ranging from barrier island beach homes to golf resort master-planned communities like Leland and St. James.

    What is the average price of a home in Brunswick County, NC?

    Real estate pricing in Brunswick County varies significantly by location and lifestyle. Mainland inland homes in Leland, Shallotte, and Bolivia range from $250,000 to $500,000. Master-planned golf and resort communities average $450,000 to $950,000, while barrier island homes on Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Oak Island, Holden Beach, and Bald Head Island range from $500,000 to over $4,000,000 for direct oceanfront or deep-water Intracoastal estates.

    What flood insurance considerations apply when buying property in Brunswick County?

    Properties in coastal North Carolina fall into distinct FEMA flood zones: Zone X (moderate/minimal risk, usually not requiring mandatory lender flood insurance), Zone AE (100-year floodplain requiring flood insurance if mortgaged), and Zone VE (coastal high-hazard velocity zones with wave action). Every buyer should request an official Elevation Certificate and consult a local insurance broker to estimate annual premiums during the due diligence period.

    Are short-term vacation rentals (STVR) permitted in Brunswick County towns?

    Yes, short-term vacation rentals are widely supported across Brunswick County barrier islands including Ocean Isle Beach, Oak Island, Holden Beach, and Sunset Beach, driving robust investment yields. However, individual municipalities and homeowners associations (HOAs) may enforce specific noise ordinances, parking limits, trash guidelines, or minimum stay durations. Always review local town codes and HOA covenants prior to purchase.

    What should I know about NC General Contractors and piling construction for coastal homes?

    Coastal barrier island homes are built on driven timber pilings anchored into deep sand to withstand storm surge and high wind loads. In NC, home builders must be licensed by the NC Licensing Board for General Contractors (NCLBGC). Homes in coastal wind zones require DP-rated impact glass, hurricane straps, corrosion-resistant stainless or hot-dipped galvanized fasteners, and elevated HVAC platforms.

    How do property taxes in Brunswick County compare to national and state averages?

    Brunswick County boasts exceptionally competitive property tax rates compared to national averages and neighboring coastal states. The base county tax rate, combined with local municipal or fire district rates, creates an attractive environment for retirees, primary homeowners, and real estate investors.
