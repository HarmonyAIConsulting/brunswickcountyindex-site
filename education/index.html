    Brunswick County Education FAQ

    Clear answers to common questions asked by families, students, and relocating residents.

    How do I enroll a new student in Brunswick County Schools?

    Enrollment for Brunswick County Schools is completed online through the official district portal (bcswan.net). Parents need to provide the child's certified birth certificate, up-to-date immunization records, proof of residency (utility bill, lease, or mortgage statement in Brunswick County), and transcripts or report cards from the previous school.

    What public high schools serve Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County Public Schools operates three main high schools based on geographic attendance zones: North Brunswick High School (serving Leland, Belville, and Navassa), South Brunswick High School (serving Southport, Oak Island, Boiling Spring Lakes, and Bolivia), and West Brunswick High School (serving Shallotte, Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, and Calabash). The district also operates the Early College High School on the BCC campus.

    What is Brunswick County Early College High School?

    Brunswick County Early College High School (ECHS) is an innovative five-year public high school program located on the main campus of Brunswick Community College in Bolivia. Students earn both a North Carolina high school diploma and up to two years of tuition-free college credit or an Associate Degree simultaneously.

    What degrees and certifications does Brunswick Community College offer?

    Brunswick Community College (BCC) offers Associate in Arts (AA), Associate in Science (AS), and Associate in Fine Arts (AFA) degrees designed for seamless transfer to four-year universities like UNCW and East Carolina. BCC also provides high-demand vocational credentials in Nursing (RN/LPN), Emergency Medical Services, Aquaculture, Welding, Cosmetology, Business Administration, Information Technology, and Electrical Line Worker training.

    Are there charter schools in Brunswick County?

    Yes. Brunswick County is home to reputable tuition-free public charter schools including Charter Day School in Leland/Supply and Classical Charter Schools of America, which offer classical education curricula and alternative learning environments open to all North Carolina residents via annual lottery admissions.

    How does homeschooling work in Brunswick County, NC?

    To homeschool in North Carolina, parents must register a Chief Administrator with the NC Division of Non-Public Education (DNPE) once the student reaches age 7. Parents must hold a high school diploma or equivalent, maintain attendance and immunization records, and administer annual standardized testing. Local co-ops and homeschool networks in Brunswick County offer enrichment classes, sports leagues, and social meetups.

    What early childhood and NC Pre-K options are available?

    Brunswick County offers multiple licensed early learning centers, private preschools, and state-funded NC Pre-K classrooms managed through the Smart Start of Brunswick County partnership. NC Pre-K provides high-quality preschool free of charge to eligible 4-year-olds based on family income and developmental indicators.

    Where are the public library branches located in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County Library operates five convenient regional branches: Rourk Library in Shallotte, Harper Library in Southport, Leland Library in Leland, Barbee Library in Oak Island, and Southwest Regional Library in Carolina Shores. All branches offer free library cards, public computer access, digital e-books, printing, and children's literacy programs.
