    ACCESSIBILITY & ACCOMMODATIONS

    Senior Accessibility Resources

    Accommodations available for seniors with mobility, vision, hearing, or cognitive needs across Brunswick County programs.

    ADA-Accessible Senior Centers

    All five BSRI senior centers are wheelchair accessible with accessible restrooms, parking, and entrances.

    Transportation Assistance

    BSRI provides door-to-door rides for seniors using mobility aids. Vehicles are equipped with wheelchair lifts.

    Large-Print Materials

    Program schedules, menus, and information materials are available in large print on request.

    Hearing Assistance

    Some senior centers offer hearing-loop-compatible meeting rooms. Contact your local center to confirm availability.

    Home-Delivered Meals

    Meals on Wheels drivers check on homebound seniors during each delivery. Special dietary needs (diabetic, low-sodium) are accommodated when possible.

    Caregiver In-Home Support

    In-home aide services can assist with bathing, dressing, and light housekeeping for eligible seniors through DSS Adult Services.

    Senior Services FAQs in Brunswick County

    Direct answers to common questions about senior care, housing, transportation, and caregiver resources.

    What senior services are available in Brunswick County, NC?

    Brunswick County offers comprehensive senior services through Brunswick Senior Resources Inc. (BSRI), which operates five senior centers across the county (Supply, Shallotte, Southport, Leland, and Calabash). Services include Meals on Wheels delivery, congregate dining, medical transportation, caregiver respite, wellness programs, fitness classes, and case management. Additional services are available through the Brunswick County Health Department, Novant Health, and Dosher Memorial Hospital.

    Source: Brunswick Senior Resources Inc.Brunswick Senior Resources Inc.Reviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I find assisted living or memory care facilities in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County Index maintains a directory of licensed assisted living communities, memory care facilities, and independent living communities. You can filter by community, care level, and amenities. Key facilities include those in Southport, Shallotte, Leland, and Supply. We recommend touring multiple communities, checking NC Division of Health Service Regulation licensing records, and consulting with a senior care advisor.

    Source: NC DHSR Facility Star RatingsNC Division of Health Service RegulationReviewed 2026-07-01

    Does Brunswick County offer Meals on Wheels for homebound seniors?

    Yes. Brunswick Senior Resources Inc. (BSRI) operates the Meals on Wheels program delivering over 150,000 hot meals annually to homebound seniors across Brunswick County. The program operates from five senior center locations and relies on volunteer drivers. To enroll a senior or volunteer, contact BSRI at (910) 754-2300 or visit their Supply headquarters at 2471 Supply Rd SE.

    Source: Brunswick Senior Resources Inc.Brunswick Senior Resources Inc.Reviewed 2026-07-01

    What transportation options exist for seniors without a car in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County offers several senior transportation options: BSRI provides medical appointment transportation and grocery shopping shuttles; Brunswick County Public Transportation operates demand-response transit services; and volunteer driver programs through local nonprofits. Some communities also have golf cart ordinances that allow street-legal golf carts for local trips in areas like St. James and Bald Head Island.

    Source: Brunswick Senior Resources Inc.Brunswick Senior Resources Inc.Reviewed 2026-07-01

    How can I get help understanding Medicare and insurance options in Brunswick County?

    The Seniors' Health Insurance Information Program (SHIIP), operated by the NC Department of Insurance, provides free unbiased Medicare counseling through trained volunteers at senior centers across Brunswick County. SHIIP counselors help compare Part D prescription plans, Medigap policies, Medicare Advantage plans, and long-term care insurance. Contact BSRI or your local senior center to schedule a free consultation.

    Source: NC Department of Insurance — SHIIPNC Department of InsuranceReviewed 2026-07-01

    Are there caregiver support groups in Brunswick County?

    Yes. Brunswick County offers multiple caregiver support resources: BSRI hosts monthly caregiver support groups at their senior centers; the Alzheimer's Association maintains a Southeastern NC chapter with local support groups; Dosher Memorial Hospital and Novant Health offer caregiver education programs; and the Area Agency on Aging through the Eastern Carolina Council of Governments provides regional caregiver resources and respite care grants.

    Source: Area Agency on AgingEastern Carolina Council of GovernmentsReviewed 2026-07-01
