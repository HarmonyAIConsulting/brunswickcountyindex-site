    The Ultimate Guide to Moving to Brunswick County, NC

    Everything you need to know about relocating to Brunswick County — neighborhoods, utility setup, property taxes, vehicle registration, and coastal living transitions.

    Overview & Coastal Context

    Brunswick County is the southernmost coastal county in North Carolina, spanning over 1,050 square miles between Wilmington, NC and Myrtle Beach, SC. It encompasses 20 municipalities, including the barrier island communities of Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, Caswell Beach, and Bald Head Island, along with mainland towns like Shallotte, Southport, Leland, Bolivia, and Calabash.

    The county is one of the fastest-growing in North Carolina, attracting retirees, families, and visitors drawn to its 45 miles of coastline, 30+ golf courses, historic lighthouses, and affordable coastal lifestyle.

    Key Fact

    Brunswick County is one of the fastest-growing counties in North Carolina, maintaining low tax rates while expanding healthcare, educational, and public infrastructure.

    Source: Brunswick County Government

    Step 1: Selecting the Right Brunswick County Town

    Every town in Brunswick County offers a distinct lifestyle and personality. Understanding these differences is the first step in choosing where to settle.

    • Island Beach Communities: Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, and Bald Head Island offer pristine beach life and vacation rental potential.
    • Historic Coastal Towns: Southport and Calabash feature waterfront dining, antique shops, colonial history, and maritime heritage.
    • Rapid Growth & Amenities: Leland, Shallotte, and Belville provide suburban conveniences, modern shopping centers, and quick access to Wilmington.

    Step 2: Utility, Water & Internet Setup

    Brunswick County Public Utilities manages municipal water and sewer for most unincorporated areas and participating towns. Electric power is supplied by Brunswick Electric Membership Corporation (BEMC) or Duke Energy Progress depending on location.

    • Water & Sewer: Brunswick County Public Utilities: (910) 253-2657
    • Electric Power: BEMC: (800) 842-5871 | Duke Energy: (800) 452-2777
    Key Fact

    Brunswick County Public Utilities provides water and sewer service to most unincorporated areas and participating municipalities.

    Source: Brunswick County Public Utilities

    Step 3: Driver's License & Vehicle Registration

    New North Carolina residents must register vehicles with the NC DMV within 30 days and obtain an NC driver's license within 60 days. DMV license renewal and testing offices are located in Shallotte and Leland.

    Key Fact

    New NC residents must register vehicles within 30 days and obtain a driver license within 60 days of establishing residency.

    Source: North Carolina DMV

    Step 4: Property Taxes, Flood Zones & Insurance

    Brunswick County sets a base county property tax rate, while incorporated towns add a municipal rate. Property tax assessments are handled by the Brunswick County Tax Office in Bolivia, NC. Coastal properties may require flood insurance depending on FEMA flood zone designation.

    Key Fact

    Property tax in Brunswick County combines a county base rate with a municipal rate for incorporated towns.

    Source: Brunswick County Tax Office
