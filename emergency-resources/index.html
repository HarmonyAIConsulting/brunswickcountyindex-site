    JUNE 1 – NOVEMBER 30

    Hurricane Season Preparedness

    Brunswick County's coastal location makes hurricane preparedness essential. Know your zone, routes, and shelters before a storm approaches.

    STEP 1

    Know Your Zone

    Check your evacuation zone at knowyourzone.nc.gov. Brunswick County includes zones A, B, C, and D with different evacuation timing.

    STEP 2

    Build Your Kit

    Prepare 3-7 days of water, food, medications, documents, and supplies. Include pet needs and phone chargers.

    STEP 3

    Plan Your Route

    Primary routes: US-17 North, NC-211 North, NC-130 West. Know your bridge off the barrier islands.

    STEP 4

    Stay Informed

    Sign up for ReadyBrunswick alerts. Monitor NOAA Weather Radio and local news for storm updates.

    BARRIER ISLAND ACCESS

    Hurricane Re-Entry Permits

    Barrier island property owners need a re-entry permit to return after an evacuation. Apply before hurricane season — do not wait until a storm is approaching.

    STEP 1

    Apply Before Storm Season

    Barrier island property owners should obtain re-entry permits from their town hall before June 1. Required for Ocean Isle Beach, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Oak Island, and Bald Head Island.

    Contact your town hall
    STEP 2

    Bring Required Documents

    Present your re-entry permit, government-issued photo ID, and proof of property ownership or residency (utility bill, tax record, or lease) at the checkpoint.

    Gather documents now
    STEP 3

    Wait for All-Clear

    Re-entry is permitted only after the town issues an all-clear following safety inspections. Checkpoints are staffed by local police. Priority re-entry is given to emergency personnel and utility crews.

    Monitor town alerts

    Emergency Resources FAQs

    Direct answers about hurricane preparedness, emergency contacts, and disaster resources in Brunswick County.

    What are the hurricane evacuation routes for Brunswick County, NC?

    Brunswick County has designated primary evacuation routes: US-17 North (toward Wilmington), NC-211 North (toward Bolton), and NC-130 West (toward Whiteville). Barrier island residents must use the single bridges connecting to the mainland: Ocean Isle Beach via NC-904, Sunset Beach via NC-179, Holden Beach via NC-130, Oak Island via NC-133/211, and Bald Head Island via passenger ferry to Southport. Evacuation orders are issued by Brunswick County Emergency Management and broadcast through the ReadyBrunswick alert system.

    Source: Brunswick County Emergency ManagementBrunswick County Emergency ManagementReviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I sign up for Brunswick County emergency alerts?

    Register for the ReadyBrunswick emergency notification system at brunswickcountync.gov/alerts. You can receive alerts via text message, email, and phone call for hurricane warnings, evacuation orders, boil water advisories, road closures, and other emergency events. We recommend signing up before hurricane season begins on June 1st.

    Source: Brunswick County Emergency ManagementBrunswick County Emergency ManagementReviewed 2026-07-01

    Where are the emergency shelters in Brunswick County?

    Brunswick County designates emergency shelters during hurricanes and disasters. Primary shelter locations include South Brunswick High School (Southport), North Brunswick High School (Leland), West Brunswick High School (Shallotte), and Town Creek Middle School (Winnabow). Shelter openings are announced through local media, the ReadyBrunswick system, and the Brunswick County Emergency Management website. Pet-friendly shelter accommodations are available at designated locations.

    Source: Brunswick County Emergency ManagementBrunswick County Emergency ManagementReviewed 2026-07-01

    What hospitals provide 24/7 emergency care in Brunswick County?

    Two hospitals provide 24/7 emergency departments: Novant Health Brunswick Medical Center at 240 Hospital Dr, Supply, NC (910-721-1000) and Dosher Memorial Hospital at 924 N Howe St, Southport, NC (910-457-3800). For ultra-specialized trauma care, patients are transferred to New Hanover Regional Medical Center in Wilmington or Grand Strand Medical Center in Myrtle Beach, SC.

    Source: Novant Health Brunswick Medical CenterNovant Health Brunswick Medical CenterReviewed 2026-07-01

    What should I include in a Brunswick County hurricane emergency kit?

    A complete hurricane kit should include: 1 gallon of water per person per day for 3-7 days, non-perishable food for 3-7 days, manual can opener, first aid kit, flashlight and extra batteries, NOAA weather radio, prescription medications for 2 weeks, cash, important documents in waterproof container, pet supplies, phone chargers and power banks, and a full gas tank. Brunswick County residents should also have re-entry permits and know their evacuation zone.

    Source: Ready.gov / FEMAU.S. Department of Homeland SecurityReviewed 2026-07-01

    How do I report a power outage or downed power line in Brunswick County?

    Report power outages to Duke Energy at 1-800-769-3766 or through the Duke Energy mobile app. For downed power lines, call 911 immediately — never approach or drive over a downed line. Brunswick County Public Utilities handles water and sewer emergencies at (910) 253-2657. Check the Duke Energy outage map for real-time restoration estimates.

    Source: Duke EnergyDuke EnergyReviewed 2026-07-01
